# consciousness as retribution
> 校准自「maturation consciousness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vipāka-vijñāna*

## Explanation（English）
One of the alternate names of the eighth, ālaya-consciousness, insofar as it is the body of total retribution induced by good and bad karma—hence retribution-consciousness.
