# noncraving
> 校准自「non-greed」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*alobha*

## Explanation（English）
One of the good mental activities among the mental activities. Non-craving is the mind free of greedy clinging toward wealth, sensual pleasure, fame, and profit. Cheng weishi lun says: “What is noncraving? Toward existence and the requisites of existence, non-attachment as nature; counteracting greedy clinging and doing good as function.” Existence means the three existences—desire, form, and formless—that is, the three realms of desire, form, and formlessness, the abodes where sentient beings live; the requisites of existence are the causes of birth in the three existences, namely passion and karma. Non-craving is not clinging greedily to the abodes of sentient existence, and not creating passion and karma.
