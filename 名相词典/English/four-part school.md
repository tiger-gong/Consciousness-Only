# four-part school
> 校准自「four-portion school」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

## Explanation（English）
The doctrine of the four parts of consciousness is regarded as the Consciousness-Only orthodox view. Among the Ten Great Treatise-masters: Sthiramati posited only the self-authenticating part (one-part school); Nanda posited the perceiving and seen parts (two-part school); Dignāga posited perceiving, image, and self-authenticating parts (three-part school); Dharmapāla posited perceiving, image, self-aware, and part that authenticates self-authentications (four-part school). In perfuming, the seeing part perfumes cognizing seeds, the seen part perfumes cognized seeds, and the substance part also perfumes; the part that authenticates self-authentication has no perfuming efficacy. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 2.
