# Pratyekabuddha
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Pratyekabuddha*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Pratyekabuddha, also “solitary-awakened.” Awakening to the Way by contemplating the twelve links of dependent origination, such a one mostly cultivates and realizes alone in an age without a Buddha; one of the two vehicles.
