# eastern land of Videha
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Videha*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Videha, an abbreviation for the eastern continent among the four great continents (Pūrvavideha), and also referring to the ancient eastern Indian kingdom of Videha.
