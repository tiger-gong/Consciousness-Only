# hatred
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*dveṣa*

## Explanation（English）
A root affliction among the mental factors. Hatred is the mental function of aversion toward suffering and the causes of suffering, with the wish to inflict harm. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is hatred? Its nature is aversion toward suffering and the supports of suffering; it obstructs nonhatred, has the quality of unrest, and has the activity of serving as the basis of evil conduct.' Hatred obstructs nonhatred, agitates body and mind with burning distress, and on that basis one performs unwholesome deeds.
