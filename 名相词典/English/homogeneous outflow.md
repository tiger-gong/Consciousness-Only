# homogeneous outflow
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*niṣyanda*

## Explanation（English）
“Homogeneous” (deng) means “same as”; “outflow” (liu) means “of the same class.” “Homogeneous outflow” thus means “of one and the same class”—simply put, when the fruit is of the same kind as its cause, it is called homogeneous outflow. Fascicle 21 of the Abhidharmakośa says: “Shamelessness, stinginess, and restlessness are the homogeneous outflow of greed”—meaning that shamelessness and the rest flow out from greed, since shamelessness and the like all possess a nature homogeneous with “greed,” hence called homogeneous outflow.
