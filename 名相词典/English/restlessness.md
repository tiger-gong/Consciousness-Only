# restlessness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*auddhatya*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Restlessness is mental unsettledness—agitated floating excitement that obstructs meditative concentration. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is restlessness? Causing the mind toward objects to be not tranquil as nature; it obstructs equanimity and śamatha as activity.' Śamatha is 'calm abiding,' i.e., concentration. See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
