# non-hatred
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*adveṣa*

## Explanation（English）
One of the eleven wholesome mental factors among the mental factors. Non-hatred is not giving rise to anger toward suffering and the causes of suffering. Cheng weishi lun says: “What is non-hatred? Toward suffering and the causes of suffering, non-anger as nature; counteracting hatred and doing good as function.” Suffering refers to the oppressions of the three sufferings and the like; the causes of suffering are the conditions that can produce suffering. Non-hatred functions as kindness and compassion, able to give sentient beings ease and joy, counteracting hatred and assisting all goods.
