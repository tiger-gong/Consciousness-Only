# sloth
> 校准自「sleep (drowsiness)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*middha*

## Explanation（English）
A nondetermined mental activity. Sleep is slumber: dull and not free, obstructing wisdom. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'Sleep means slumber: causing the body to be not free, dull and abbreviated as nature; it obstructs insight as activity.' In sleep the mind is extremely dark and weak, the body without functional power, thus obstructing (calm and) insight. Moderate sleep is wholesome; excessive sleep is unwholesome. See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
