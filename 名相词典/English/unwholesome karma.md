# unwholesome karma
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*akuśala-karma*

## Explanation（English）
Of the actions of body, speech, and mind, those rooted in craving, hatred, and delusion, and that harm this life and other lives, are called unwholesome karma. It stands opposite [[English/wholesome karma|wholesome karma]]: the one draws an agreeable [[English/karmic result|karmic result]], the other a disagreeable result. Karma already made is held in the [[English/store consciousness|store consciousness]]; it is not cancelled by later wholesome deeds, but can be weakened by [[English/repentance|repentance]] and the like. The ten unwholesome paths are the list most often cited; they do not exhaust unwholesome karma.
