# King Prasenajit
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Prasenajit*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Prasenajit, king of Kośala in central India, a contemporary of the Buddha who took refuge in the Three Jewels and was an important protector of Buddhism. His questioning of the Dharma is recorded in the Śūraṅgama-sūtra and elsewhere.
