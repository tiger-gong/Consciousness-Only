# fivefold contemplation of consciousness-only

## Explanation（English）
Contemplative stages established by Kuiji and others: discard the false and retain the real; abandon the mixed and keep the pure; gather the branches back to the root; conceal the inferior and reveal the superior; discard marks and realize the nature—progressing from shallow to deep into the true nature of consciousness-only.
