# pliancy (ease)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*praśrabdhi*

## Explanation（English）
One of the wholesome mental factors among the mental factors. Ease and lightness of body and mind is called pliancy. Cheng weishi lun says: “Ease means pliancy: free from coarseness-and-heaviness, regulating and refreshing body and mind, fitness for the task as nature; counteracting dullness and transformation of the basis as function.” Coarseness-and-heaviness refers to the afflictions of greed and delusion; the practitioner subdues afflictions and leaves coarseness-and-heaviness—this is a necessary condition for cultivating meditative concentration. “Transformation of the basis as function” means turning away from coarseness-and-heaviness and relying on pliancy. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
