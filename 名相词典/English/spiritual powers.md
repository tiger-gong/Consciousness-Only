# spiritual powers
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*ṛddhi*

## Explanation（English）
Also called the power of spiritual penetration, spiritual power, or penetrative power. It is the unobstructed, sovereign, superhuman, and inconceivable functioning obtained by cultivating meditative concentration. There are five spiritual penetrations—the base of spiritual power, the divine eye, the divine ear, knowledge of others' minds, and knowledge of former lives—which, together with the penetration of the extinction of outflows, make six. (1) The penetration of the base of spiritual power (also “penetration realized by knowledge of the spiritual sphere,” “bodily sovereign penetration,” etc.): according to fascicles 5 and 28 of the Da zhidu lun, it is of three kinds—moving at will, able to fly anywhere; transforming at will, changing appearances; and the “sagely sovereignty” of freely transforming the external objects (the six objects), the last of which only a Buddha possesses. (2) The divine eye (penetration realized by the knowledge of the divine eye): the function of seeing through all things far and near, painful and pleasant, coarse and fine, in the world. (3) The divine ear (penetration realized by the knowledge of the divine ear): the function of hearing all sounds in the world. (4) Knowledge of others' minds (penetration realized by the knowledge of others' minds): the power to know all the good, evil, and other thoughts in others' minds. (5) Knowledge of former lives (penetration realized by mindful recollection of former abodes): the function of knowing all the past-life conditions of oneself and others. (6) The extinction of outflows (penetration realized by the knowledge of the extinction of outflows): the awakened power that severs the afflictions completely so as never to be reborn in the deluded realm. Of these, the three penetrations possessed by Buddhas and arhats (knowledge of former lives, the divine eye, the extinction of outflows) are the most excellent, hence called the three insights; the divine eye, which thoroughly sees the times and circumstances of the death and rebirth of beings in future lives, is called the insight of the knowledge of death-and-rebirth. According to fascicle 27 of the Abhidharmakośa, all six penetrations have wisdom as their essential nature; five of them are obtained by cultivating the four dhyānas and are not the exclusive possession of the sages—ordinary beings too may obtain them—but the penetration of the extinction of outflows can be obtained by the sages alone. Fascicle 28 of the Da zhidu lun says bodhisattvas have five penetrations and Buddhas have six. Fascicle 16 of the Chengshi lun points out that outer-path practitioners outside Buddhism can also obtain the five penetrations; a sage-seer who obtains the five is called a five-penetration seer. Moreover, the penetrative powers of ghosts, animals, gods, and the like are of two sorts—innate (obtained at birth) and cultivated (obtained by cultivating meditation). Fascicle 20a of the Dasheng yizhang records four methods of obtaining spiritual penetration: (1) the retribution-penetration obtained naturally as the fruit of birth in the four dhyāna heavens; (2) the karma-penetration by which seers freely fly through the air through the power of karma; (3) the mantra-penetration obtained by brahmins through holding spells; (4) the cultivation-penetration obtained by cultivating meditative concentration—of which the karma-penetration and mantra-penetration belong to the outer paths.
