# Mahābrahmā
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Mahābrahmā*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Mahābrahmā, lord of the first-dhyāna heaven of the form realm, named Śikhin. Outer paths often mistake him for the creator of the world, whereas Buddhism regards him as a Dharma-protecting deity still within the cycle of birth-and-death.
