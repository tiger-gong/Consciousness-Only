# Chapter on the Fundamental Stage
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Maulī-bhūmi*

## Explanation（English）
The first of the five parts of the Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra, expounding at length the objects and characteristics of the “seventeen stages”; it is the main body and foundation of the whole treatise, and the enumeration of the hundred dharmas in the Treatise on the Hundred Dharmas is drawn from it.
