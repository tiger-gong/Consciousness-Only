# Queen Mallikā
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Mallikā*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Mallikā, queen of King Prasenajit, a devout believer in the Buddha-Dharma. Because she observed the fast and precepts and urged the king toward the good, she was praised by the Buddha as a model laywoman.
