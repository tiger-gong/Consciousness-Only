# carefulness (non-laxity)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*apramāda*

## Explanation（English）
One of the fifty-one mental factors of the Consciousness-Only school; the opposite of carelessness. The mental function that guards against evils and focuses on cultivating the good is called carefulness. The Abhidharma-kośa school takes carefulness as the mental factor for the arising of all wholesome minds; the Consciousness-Only school likewise takes it as a wholesome mental factor. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
