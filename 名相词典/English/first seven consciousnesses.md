# first seven consciousnesses

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the first six consciousnesses plus the seventh, the manas—seven in all. All seven arise from the eighth, ālaya, consciousness and are also called the seven evolving consciousnesses, set in contrast to the eighth consciousness which is their root.
