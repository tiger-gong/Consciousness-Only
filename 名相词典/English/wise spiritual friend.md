# wise spiritual friend
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kalyāṇamitra*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit kalyāṇamitra, referring to a good teacher and beneficial friend who can instruct in the true Dharma, lead one toward the good, and help one accomplish the practice.
