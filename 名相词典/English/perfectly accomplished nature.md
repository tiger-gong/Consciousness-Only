# perfectly accomplished nature
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pariniṣpanna-svabhāva*

## Explanation（English）
Also called the perfectly accomplished characteristic; one of the three self-natures of Consciousness-Only. It means that suchness has three qualities—perfect, accomplished, and real: perfect, in that the wondrous principle of suchness pervades everywhere; accomplished, in that the true substance of suchness abides constantly without arising or ceasing; real, in that the nature of suchness abides constantly and pervades thoroughly. The perfectly accomplished nature is the truth revealed by emptying the two hindrances of affliction and of the knowable—that is, the true essential nature of all dharmas; it is also named suchness, true characteristic, Dharma-realm, and Dharma-nature. The true nature of all dharmas revealed by the emptiness of self and the emptiness of dharmas is the perfectly accomplished nature. Upon the other-dependent nature, constantly free from the imagined nature—that is the perfectly accomplished nature.
