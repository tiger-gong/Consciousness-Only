# anger (fury)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*krodha*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Anger is furious indignation: toward a present adverse object, it produces coarse violent bodily and verbal expressions. The Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6, says: 'What is anger? Relying on a present unbeneficial object, outburst as nature; it obstructs non-anger and has the activity of taking up weapons—for those who harbor anger often emit violent evil bodily expressions.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
