# wholesome karma
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kuśala-karma*

## Explanation（English）
Of the actions of body, speech, and mind, those rooted in non-craving, non-hatred, and non-delusion, and that bring favorable benefit in this life and in other lives, are called wholesome karma. This is not the same as the [[English/good mental activities|good mental activities]]: those are the eleven mental activities that turn only with a wholesome mind; wholesome karma is the deed thus produced, which can draw an agreeable [[English/karmic result|karmic result]]. The ten wholesome paths (three of body, four of speech, three of mind) are the list most often cited; they do not exhaust wholesome karma. The counterpart is [[English/unwholesome karma|unwholesome karma]].
