# theory of wrong causation
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*mithyā-hetu*

## Explanation（English）
A mistaken view of causation held by outer paths, asserting that all things are produced by wrong, unequal, and incongruous causes (such as Maheśvara, time, or space); it is not the correct causation of the Buddha-Dharma.
