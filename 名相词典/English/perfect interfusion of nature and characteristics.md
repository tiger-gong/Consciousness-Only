# perfect interfusion of nature and characteristics

## Explanation（English）
Means that the essence-nature (nature) and the characteristics (mark) of all dharmas are originally unobstructed, mutually embracing and interfusing, neither identical nor different. The Huayan and Tiantai schools often use it to reveal the principle of the non-duality of the ultimate and conventional.
