# ten dharma-realms

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the four noble dharma-realms—Buddhas, bodhisattvas, pratyekabuddhas, and śrāvakas—together with the six ordinary dharma-realms of gods, humans, asuras, animals, hungry ghosts, and hells; jointly called the ten dharma-realms, they encompass all realms of delusion and awakening.
