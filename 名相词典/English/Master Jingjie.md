# Master Jingjie

## Explanation（English）
A contemporary Chinese Buddhist master who lectures on Consciousness-Only, Tiantai, and Pure Land studies; the present course, the Direct Explanation of the Treatise on the Hundred Dharmas, was compiled from his lectures.
