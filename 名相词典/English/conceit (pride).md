# conceit (pride)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*māna*

## Explanation（English）
One of the seven conceits. Comparing oneself with others in superiority or inferiority, excellence or deficiency, good or bad, and thereby arising a self-assertive mind that belittles others is called conceit—that is, contempt and self-importance. The Yogācāra school teaches seven conceits: regarding those inferior to oneself as surpassed by oneself—'deeming oneself superior to the inferior'; regarding equals, still belittling them—'deeming oneself equal among equals.' See the entry 'seven conceits.'
