# dharma means rule and retention

## Explanation（English）
An explanation of the word “dharma”: dharma has two senses—“serving as a norm that gives rise to understanding” and “maintaining its own nature.” As a norm it enables one to form cognition; by “maintaining” it preserves each thing's own essence—hence it is called dharma.
