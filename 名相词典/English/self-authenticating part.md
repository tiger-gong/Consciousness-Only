# self-authenticating part
> 校准自「self-aware portion」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*svasaṃvitti-bhāga*

## Explanation（English）
Also called the substance part; the third of the four parts of consciousness. The seeing part can cognize and discern the seen part, but cannot know of itself whether what it sees is erroneous; hence there must be a function that verifies the seeing part—namely the self-authenticating part. The self-authenticating part is the substance of consciousness. The self-authenticating part and the part that authenticates self-authentication verify each other.
