# mental consciousness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*mano-vijñāna*

## Explanation（English）
The sixth consciousness, arising based on the mental faculty (manas), taking dharma-objects as its object. It has functions of discrimination, comparison, inference, imagination, etc.; extends over the three natures and is the chief agent of karma; it can also take past and future as objects and pervades all dharmas.
