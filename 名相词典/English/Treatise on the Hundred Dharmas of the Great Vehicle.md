# Treatise on the Hundred Dharmas of the Great Vehicle
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Mahāyāna Śatadharma-vidyādvāra Śāstra*

## Explanation（English）
In one fascicle; composed by the treatise-master Vasubandhu, translated by the Tang Tripiṭaka-master Xuanzang. It is one of the “one root and ten branch” texts of the Consciousness-Only school—also called the “branch that briefly sets out names and numbers”—taking “all dharmas are without self” as its tenet. The treatise draws the enumeration of the hundred dharmas from the “Fundamental Stage” chapter of the Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra, and is one of the important texts on which the Consciousness-Only school relies. The whole work briefly divides all dharmas into the Five Categories and Hundred Dharmas: (1) mind-king dharmas, the eight consciousnesses; (2) mental-factor dharmas, six divisions such as the universally active, comprising fifty-one factors; (3) form dharmas, eleven kinds such as the eye; (4) dharmas not concomitant with mind, twenty-four kinds such as “attainment”; (5) unconditioned dharmas, six kinds such as unconditioned space.
