# five aggregates
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pañca-skandha*

## Explanation（English）
The five aggregates—form, feeling, perception, formations, and consciousness—summarizing the body-mind of sentient beings. The form aggregate is material; the latter four are mental. The Buddha taught that the five aggregates are without self.
