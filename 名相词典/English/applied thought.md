# applied thought
> 校准自「initial inquiry (vitarka)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vitarka*

## Explanation（English）
A nondetermined mental activity. Initial inquiry is seeking: coarse reflection on principles and phenomena. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'Inquiry means seeking: causing the mind to be hurried, toward mental-verbal objects, coarse turning as nature.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
