# denying cause and effect

## Explanation（English）
The wrong view that denies karmic cause and effect—holding that doing good does not bring a pleasant retribution and doing evil does not bring a painful one. It belongs to nihilism and is one of the major wrong views refuted by the Buddha-Dharma.
