# authoritative sacred word
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*āptavacana*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit āptavacana, one of the three means of knowledge in epistemology: taking the verbal teaching of the Buddha and other sages as a reliable basis for cognition—set in contrast to direct perception (pratyakṣa) and inference (anumāna).
