# instant / moment
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kṣaṇa*

## Explanation（English）
Translated as “a single thought-moment,” it is the smallest unit of time. The Huayan tanxuan ji, fascicle 18, says: “A kṣaṇa is here called a thought-instant; within the span of a finger-snap there are sixty kṣaṇas.” Xuanzang's Records of the Western Regions of the Great Tang, fascicle 2, says: “The shortest span of time is the kṣaṇa; 120 kṣaṇas make one tatkṣaṇa, 60 tatkṣaṇas make one lava, 30 lavas make one muhūrta, 5 muhūrtas make one kāla (‘period’), and six periods complete one day-and-night.” Converted into modern reckoning, one day-and-night equals 6,480,000 kṣaṇas, one hour 270,000 kṣaṇas, one minute 4,500 kṣaṇas, and one second 75 kṣaṇas.
