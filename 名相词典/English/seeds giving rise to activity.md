# seeds giving rise to activity
> 校准自「seeds giving rise to present activity」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。


## Explanation（English）
When seeds in the ālaya-consciousness meet conditions and give rise to the present-activity fruit of the dharmas of form and mind, this is called seeds giving rise to activity. Seeds are the cause, activity the effect; at the same time activity can perfume new seeds stored back in the ālaya-consciousness, forming the continuous mutual succession of seeds and activity.
