# kingdom of Ayodhyā
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Ayodhyā*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Ayodhyā, an ancient kingdom of central India, also written Ayodhya. Asaṅga and Vasubandhu propagated the Dharma there, and it is said that the bodhisattva Maitreya expounded the Five Great Treatises for Asaṅga in this land.
