# resentment
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*upanāha*

## Explanation（English）
The second of the minor concomitant afflictions among the mental factors. Resentment is grudging enmity: harboring ill will without letting go, causing extreme affliction. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is resentment? Preceded by anger, harboring ill without relinquishing, binding enmity as nature; it obstructs non-resentment and has the activity of burning distress—for those who bind resentment cannot forbear and are constantly heated with affliction.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
