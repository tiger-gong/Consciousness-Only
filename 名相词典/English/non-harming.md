# non-harming
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*avihiṃsā*

## Explanation（English）
Non-harming means not killing or injuring any living being. In the Consciousness-Only school it is one of the eleven wholesome mental factors; non-harming has non-hatred as nature, and toward sentient beings does no injury. Cheng weishi lun says: “What is non-harming? Toward sentient beings, not causing injury or vexation, with non-hatred as nature; able to counteract harming, with compassion as function.” Non-harming is the opposite of harming that injures sentient beings; non-hatred is kindness, giving beings joy; non-harming is compassion, relieving beings of suffering. Non-harming is provisionally established upon the function of non-hatred and is not posited as a separate substance. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
