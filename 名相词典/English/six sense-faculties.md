# six sense-faculties
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*ṣaḍ-indriya*

## Explanation（English）
The six faculties supporting sensory cognition: eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, and mind. The first five are material faculties; the mental faculty is a mental dharma (manas).
