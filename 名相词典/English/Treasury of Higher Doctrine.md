# Treasury of Higher Doctrine
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Abhidharmakośa*

## Explanation（English）
Composed by the bodhisattva Vasubandhu, translated by Xuanzang; in thirty fascicles. Abbreviated the Kośa, it summarizes and critically examines the doctrines of the Sarvāstivāda, establishing the Five Categories and Seventy-five Dharmas; it is the representative treatise of sectarian Buddhism.
