# envy
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*īrṣyā*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Envy is jealousy: when others prosper, one harbors distress. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is envy? Pursuing one's own fame and gain, intolerant of others' glory, jealousy as nature; it obstructs non-envy and has the activity of sorrow—for the envious, hearing or seeing others' prosperity, deeply harbor distress.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
