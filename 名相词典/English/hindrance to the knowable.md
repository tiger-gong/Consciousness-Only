# hindrance to the knowable
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*jñeyāvaraṇa*

## Explanation（English）
Rooted in grasping at dharmas, it obstructs bodhi (awakening) and screens the objects that ought to be known so they cannot be known as they truly are—called the hindrance to the knowable. Bodhisattvas must sever it to become buddhas; the two vehicles do not.
