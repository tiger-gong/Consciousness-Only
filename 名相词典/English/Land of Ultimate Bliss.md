# Land of Ultimate Bliss
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Sukhāvatī*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Sukhāvatī, the Pure Land of Amitābha Buddha, in the west. Beings who are mindful of the Buddha and make the vow can be reborn there, where they are forever free of all suffering and experience only bliss—hence “Ultimate Bliss.”
