# resultant transformer

## Explanation（English）
Counterpart of the causal transformer. It refers to the eight consciousnesses arising from seeds: from the substance part of each of the eight, the image and seeing parts are each manifested. Causal transformation is seeds giving rise to the substances of the eight consciousnesses; resultant transformation is those substances manifesting the image and seeing parts; the two transform simultaneously, yet are two transformations in meaning. Concomitant mental activities likewise each manifest image and seeing parts.
