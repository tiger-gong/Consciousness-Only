# Venerable Piṇḍola
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Piṇḍola*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Piṇḍola, a disciple of the Buddha and one of the sixteen arhats. Because he displayed spiritual powers, he was rebuked by the Buddha and made to remain long in the world without entering nirvāṇa, so as to serve as a field of merit for beings of the final age.
