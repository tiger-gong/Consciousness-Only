# maturation (ripened result)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vipāka*

## Explanation（English）
Maturation of a different kind: the cause may be good or bad, the result only indeterminate; or ripening at a different time, or through transformation. Refers to the indeterminate retribution induced by karmic causes.
