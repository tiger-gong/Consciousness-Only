# all formations are impermanent

## Explanation（English）
One of the three Dharma-seals. It teaches that all conditioned, fabricated phenomena flow and change and cannot abide permanently; it is one of the fundamental teachings by which the Buddha-Dharma distinguishes the true from the false.
