# concentration
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*samādhi*

## Explanation（English）
One of the object-specific mental factors among the mental factors. Concentration is Sanskrit samādhi, rendered “right concentration.” Cheng weishi lun says: “Toward the object contemplated, it has the nature of making the mind single-pointed and undistracted, and the function of being the basis of knowledge.” Making the mind single-pointed means mental force constantly gathered, not dispersing with the object; thus single-pointed mental force is its self-nature. Because the mind is concentrated, clear wisdom arises—that is its function. A distracted mind cannot give rise to concentration, nor has any supporting knowledge. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
