# Three Jewels
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Triratna*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Triratna, referring to the three honorable and precious ones: the Buddha (the awakened one), the Dharma (the teaching and its truth), and the Saṅgha (the pure community that practices the Dharma); they are the objects of refuge for Buddhists.
