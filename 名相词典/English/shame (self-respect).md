# shame (self-respect)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*hrī*

## Explanation（English）
One of the wholesome mental factors among the mental factors. Shame is the sense of abhorring evil: feeling shame within for having done wrong is called shame. Cheng weishi lun says: “What is shame? Relying on the power of self and of the Dharma, it has esteeming the worthy and the good as nature; counteracting shamelessness and stopping evil conduct as function.” Through the promoting force of self-respect, evil conduct can be stopped. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
