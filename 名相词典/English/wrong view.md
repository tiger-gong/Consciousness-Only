# wrong view
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*dṛṣṭi*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Wrong view, also called incorrect view or perverse view. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is wrong view? Toward the truths and principles, inverted investigation with defiled discernment as its nature; it obstructs right view and has the activity of inviting suffering.' It is provisionally established on the mental factor of discernment (prajñā), hence 'defiled discernment as nature.' It opens into five: satkāya-dṛṣṭi (view of the person), extreme views, nihilistic/wrong view, attachment to views, and attachment to rites and observances. See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
