# mind dharmas
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*citta*

## Explanation（English）
All dharmas in the universe may basically be divided into two great classes: form dharmas and mind dharmas. That which has the function of cognizing-and-apprehending is a mind dharma; that which lacks such function but has the function of resistance (materiality) is a form dharma. The “mind dharmas” referred to here are the first category among the Five Categories and Hundred Dharmas. There are eight mind dharmas—the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mental, manas, and ālaya consciousnesses—hence they are also called the “mind-king dharmas” or the “eight ruling minds.”
