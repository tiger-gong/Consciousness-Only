# kingdom of Puruṣapura
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Puruṣapura*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Puruṣapura, the capital of the ancient Indian kingdom of Gandhāra—present-day Peshawar in Pakistan; the native place of Asaṅga and Vasubandhu.
