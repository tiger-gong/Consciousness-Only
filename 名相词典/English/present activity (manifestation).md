# present activity (manifestation)

## Explanation（English）
Seeds are a doctrine established by the Dharma-characteristics school: latent capacities within the ālaya-consciousness are called seeds; when they function—i.e., when present activity arises—they are no longer called seeds but consciousness. Present activity is the functioning that can give rise to the distinct phenomena of form and mind. When latent seeds give rise to the dharmas of form and mind, this is called present activity. Present activity and seeds are mutually causal: seeds give rise to present activity; present activity perfumes seeds.
