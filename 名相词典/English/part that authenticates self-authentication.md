# part that authenticates self-authentication
> 校准自「re-aware portion (awareness of self-awareness)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
The fourth of the four parts of consciousness: further verifying the self-authenticating part. The self-authenticating part verifies the seeing part; the part that authenticates self-authentication verifies the self-authenticating part; and the part that authenticates self-authentication is in turn verified by the self-authenticating part—the two mutually cognize and verify each other, so there is no need to posit a fifth part.
