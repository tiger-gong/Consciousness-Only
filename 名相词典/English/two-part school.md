# two-part school
> 校准自「two-portion school」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

## Explanation（English）
The treatise-master Nanda advocated a two-part theory of mind-consciousness: the seeing part and the seen part. There are also Sthiramati’s one-part theory, Dignāga’s three-part theory, and Dharmapāla’s four-part theory. See “four parts of consciousness.”
