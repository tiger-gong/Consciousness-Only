# object-discerning transformer

## Explanation（English）
One of the three transformers, also the third transformer, fully “object-discerning transformer”—the first six consciousnesses (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mental). These six clearly discern coarse, evident objects—the six objects of form, sound, smell, taste, touch, and dharmas—hence the name. The Thirty Verses say: “Next, the third transformer, differentiated into six kinds”—“object-discerning consciousness” being the general name, divided into the six consciousnesses cognizing the six objects.
