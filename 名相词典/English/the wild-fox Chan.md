# the wild-fox Chan

## Explanation（English）
A Chan kōan. Under Chan Master Baizhang there was an old man who said that in a former life he had been a Chan master, and for answering “not falling under cause and effect” he fell into a fox's body for five hundred lives; later, changing his answer to “not blind to cause and effect,” he was freed. It is a metaphor for misunderstanding cause and effect and falling into wrong views.
