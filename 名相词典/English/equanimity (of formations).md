# equanimity (of formations)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*upekṣā*

## Explanation（English）
One of the eleven wholesome mental factors of the Consciousness-Only school; abbreviated as equanimity. It means leaving the agitation of dullness and restlessness, abiding in stillness, neither floating nor sinking, maintaining an even and upright mental function or state. This equanimity is included under the aggregate of formations among the five aggregates—hence called equanimity-of-formations, to distinguish it from feeling-equanimity included under the aggregate of feeling. Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6, states that this mental factor has the three functions of evenness, uprightness, and effortlessness. It is a provisionally established dharma as a stage upon diligence and the three roots. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
