# impure
> 校准自「contaminated (with outflows)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*sāsrava*

## Explanation（English）
Impure dharmas that have passions and defilement. “Leakage” (āsrava) is another name for passion, causing beings to leak into saṃsāra. Opposed to the pure.
