# doubt
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vicikitsā*

## Explanation（English）
The mental function of vacillating and being unable to decide regarding the principles of delusion and awakening, cause and effect—that is, indecision toward the truths of Buddhism. It is eradicated from the stage of stream-entry upward in the Hīnayāna, and from the first bhūmi upward for bodhisattvas. In Yogācāra it is one of the six root afflictions. There are two kinds: (1) latent doubt regarding principles (anuśaya); (2) indecision about whether a given matter is a proper or improper ground (sthānāsthāna).
