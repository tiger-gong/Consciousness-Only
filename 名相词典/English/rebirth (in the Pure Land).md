# rebirth (in the Pure Land)

## Explanation（English）
Refers to leaving this world after death and being reborn in a Pure Land of another quarter (usually Amitābha's Land of Ultimate Bliss). The nianfo (Buddha-recitation) practice takes faith, vow, and holding the name, seeking rebirth in the Pure Land, as its tenet.
