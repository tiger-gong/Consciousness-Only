# habit energy of equal flow
> 校准自「homogeneous-outflow residual impressions」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

## Explanation（English）
See name-and-word seeds. Residual impressions that can induce homogeneous-outflow fruits of the same class as their own nature; the direct causal condition of all dharmas.
