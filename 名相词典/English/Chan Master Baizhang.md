# Chan Master Baizhang

## Explanation（English）
The Tang-dynasty Chan master Huaihai (720–814), who drew up the Baizhang Monastic Rules and established the monastic norm “a day without work is a day without food.” The kōan of the “wild-fox Chan” and “not falling under / not blind to cause and effect” comes from his community.
