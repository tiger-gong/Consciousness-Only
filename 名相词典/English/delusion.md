# delusion
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*moha*

## Explanation（English）
A root affliction among the mental factors. Delusion, also called ignorance (avidyā), confuses principles and phenomena, disorders cause and effect, and wholly contradicts right meaning. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is delusion? Its nature is darkness and confusion regarding principles and phenomena; it obstructs nondelusion and has the activity of serving as the basis of all defilement—for by ignorance arise doubt, wrong views, greed and other afflictions, concomitant afflictions and karma, which can bring about future defiled dharmas.' Ignorance is first among the twelve links of dependent origination and the root of cyclic existence. It is dull unawareness: not understanding worldly principles or the Dharma, thereby giving rise to manifold afflictions. There are two kinds: innate ignorance, present from birth; and conceptually elaborated ignorance, acquired later through environmental conditioning.
