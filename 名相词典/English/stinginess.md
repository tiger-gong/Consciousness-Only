# stinginess
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*mātsarya*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Stinginess is miserliness: unwilling to give wealth, and secretly withholding Dharma from others—base hoarding of wealth and Dharma. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is stinginess? Attached to wealth and Dharma, unable to bestow, secret withholding as nature; it obstructs non-stinginess and has the activity of base accumulation—for the stingy mind is often base and tight, storing wealth and Dharma, unable to give.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
