# contaminated (with outflows)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*sāsrava*

## Explanation（English）
Contaminated dharmas that have afflictions and defilement. “Leakage” (āsrava) is another name for affliction, causing beings to leak into saṃsāra. Opposed to the uncontaminated.
