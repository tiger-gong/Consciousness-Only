# maturation consciousness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vipāka-vijñāna*

## Explanation（English）
One of the alternate names of the eighth, ālaya-consciousness, insofar as it is the body of total retribution induced by good and bad karma—hence maturation-consciousness.
