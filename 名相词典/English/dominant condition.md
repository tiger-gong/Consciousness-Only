# dominant condition
> 校准自「predominant condition」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*adhipati-pratyaya*

## Explanation（English）
Whatever has a strong efficacious power that can aid another dharma's arising or obstruct another dharma is a dominant condition. Its scope is the widest.
