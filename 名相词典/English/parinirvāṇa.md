# parinirvāṇa
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*parinirvāṇa*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit parinirvāṇa—entering nirvāṇa, complete extinction. It means the extinction of the afflictions and deliverance from birth-and-death, mostly referring to a Buddha or sage relinquishing the body and entering quiescence.
