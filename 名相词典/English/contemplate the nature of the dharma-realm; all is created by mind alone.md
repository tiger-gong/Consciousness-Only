# contemplate the nature of the dharma-realm; all is created by mind alone

## Explanation（English）
From the Avataṃsaka-sūtra. It teaches that one should contemplate the nature of the dharma-realm: all realms, and all cause-and-effect of sages and ordinary beings, are without exception created by the mind—a scriptural proof of the “mind-only” and “consciousness-only” meaning.
