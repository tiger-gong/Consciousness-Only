# Bodhisattva Maitreya
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Maitreya*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Maitreya, rendered “the Compassionate One.” He now dwells in Tuṣita Heaven as the bodhisattva of one-more-birth, and will in the future descend to be born and attain Buddhahood. Revered as a patriarch by the Yogācāra school, he is said to have expounded the “Five Great Treatises,” including the Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra.
