# seen part
> 校准自「image portion (seen portion)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*nimitta-bhāga*

## Explanation（English）
One of the four parts of mind dharmas: the objective appearance manifested on the mind’s own substance as what the seeing part cognizes, encompassing all so-called objective phenomena. When mind-consciousness arises it manifests the image and seeing parts: the seeing part is that which cognizes, the seen part the cognized objective appearance. The seen part is the objective appearance manifested by the form-dharma seeds of the eighth consciousness; the semblance-image is not the essential form of an outer object, but, relying on the image-part form of the eighth, a further semblance is re-manifested on the visual consciousness, which the seeing part of the visual consciousness then cognizes. Hence it is said: “What consciousness cognizes is only what consciousness transforms.”
