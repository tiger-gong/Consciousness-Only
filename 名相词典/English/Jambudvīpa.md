# Jambudvīpa
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Jambudvīpa*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Jambudvīpa, rendered “the Southern Continent of the Jambu tree,” the southern of the four great continents around Mount Sumeru—that is, the world we inhabit.
