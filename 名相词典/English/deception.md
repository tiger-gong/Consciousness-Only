# deception
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*māyā*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Deception is fraudulently claiming virtue one lacks in order to obtain fame or profit. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is deception? For the sake of gain and renown, falsely displaying virtue, craftiness as nature; it obstructs non-deception and has the activity of wrong livelihood.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
