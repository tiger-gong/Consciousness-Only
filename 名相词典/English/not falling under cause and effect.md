# not falling under cause and effect

## Explanation（English）
The wrong answer in Baizhang's wild-fox kōan: thinking that awakening to the Way means transcending cause and effect and no longer being bound by karmic retribution. This view denies cause and effect, and so called forth five hundred lives as a wild fox.
