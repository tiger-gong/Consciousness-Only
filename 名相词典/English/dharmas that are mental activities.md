# dharmas that are mental activities
> 校准自「mental-factor dharmas」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*caitta*

## Explanation（English）
Abbreviated as mental activities: the psychological functions subordinate to the mind-king and arising in correspondence with it. Among the Five Categories and Hundred Dharmas, fifty-one are established, in six divisions: universal, with specific objects, wholesome, of the passions, secondary of the passions, and indeterminate.
