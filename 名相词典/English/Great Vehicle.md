# Great Vehicle
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Mahāyāna*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Mahāyāna, meaning “the great vehicle.” Its goal is to attain Buddhahood and deliver all beings; it benefits both self and others, unites compassion and wisdom, and cultivates the six perfections and myriad practices—set in contrast to the Lesser Vehicle.
