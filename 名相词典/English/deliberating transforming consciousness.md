# deliberating transforming consciousness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Manas*

## Explanation（English）
One of the three transforming consciousnesses. Consciousness can transform and give rise to the myriad dharmas, or manifest as the subjective (perceiving portion) and objective (image portion) of inner cognition—hence “transformer.” The deliberating transformer, also the second transformer, is the seventh manas. Manas means “mind” and vijñāna “consciousness”; together they would equal “mental consciousness,” but to distinguish it from the sixth, the transliteration manas is kept. This consciousness constantly grasps the eighth's perceiving-portion as a “self” and deliberates and calculates—that is its nature and mode of activity; hence “constant scrutinizing deliberation” and “deliberating transformer.” Though the sixth also deliberates, it has interruptions, so it is not called the deliberating transformer.
