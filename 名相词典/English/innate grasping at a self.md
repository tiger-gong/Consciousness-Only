# innate grasping at a self

## Explanation（English）
Grasping at a self present from birth; it arises spontaneously without depending on wrong teachings or wrong thought, is subtle and hard to discern, and can only be gradually severed from the path of cultivation onward. The grasping at a self corresponding to the seventh consciousness is innate.
