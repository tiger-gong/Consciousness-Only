# lack of faith
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*āśraddhya*

## Explanation（English）
Among the hundred dharmas in five categories of Yogācāra, a major concomitant affliction. Lack of faith means absence of true confidence: without genuine faith, one cannot affirm all real facts and principles. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is lack of faith? Toward reality, virtue, and capacity, not enduring delight and aspiration, mental foulness as nature; it obstructs pure faith and has the activity of serving as a basis of downfall—for those lacking faith are often lazy.'
