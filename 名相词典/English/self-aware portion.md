# self-aware portion
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*svasaṃvitti-bhāga*

## Explanation（English）
Also called the substance portion; the third of the four portions of the substance of consciousness. The perceiving portion can cognize and discern the image portion, but cannot know of itself whether what it sees is erroneous; hence there must be a function that verifies the perceiving portion—namely the self-aware portion. The self-aware portion is the substance of consciousness. The self-aware portion and the re-aware portion verify each other.
