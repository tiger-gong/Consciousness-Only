# laxity (heedlessness)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pramāda*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Laxity is dissolute negligence: unable to guard against defiled dharmas, unwilling to cultivate pure ones. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is laxity? Toward defiled and pure classes, unable to prevent or cultivate, indulgence as nature; it obstructs non-laxity and has the activity of serving as the basis for increasing evil and diminishing good.'
