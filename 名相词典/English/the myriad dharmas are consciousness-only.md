# the myriad dharmas are consciousness-only
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vijñapti-mātra*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit vijñapti-mātra, the fundamental thesis of the Consciousness-Only school: that all existence in the universe is a manifestation of the inner consciousness, and apart from consciousness there is no independent, real object.
