# non-delusion
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*amoha*

## Explanation（English）
One of the wholesome mental factors among the mental factors. Non-delusion is clear comprehension of principles and facts, free from confusion. Cheng weishi lun says: “What is non-delusion? Toward principles and facts, clear understanding as nature; counteracting delusion and doing good as function.” Clear understanding of principles and facts means clearly grasping Buddhist teachings such as the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path.
