# perceiving portion (seeing portion)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*darśana-bhāga*

## Explanation（English）
One of the four portions of the substance of consciousness. It refers to the cognizing function of the consciousnesses—the subject that knows things, able to illuminate and know the cognitive object (the image portion). “Perceiving” means illuminating-seeing, the mind’s nature being clear. When mind-consciousness arises, from its own substance it manifests the image and perceiving portions: the image portion is form dharma, the perceiving portion is mind dharma; both are manifested by the substance of consciousness, gathering objects back into mind—hence Consciousness-Only is established.
