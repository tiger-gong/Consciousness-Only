# indifference
> 校准自「equanimity (of formations)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*upekṣā*

## Explanation（English）
One of the eleven good mental activities of the Consciousness-Only school; abbreviated as equanimity. It means leaving the agitation of dullness and agitation, abiding in stillness, neither floating nor sinking, maintaining an even and upright mental function or state. This equanimity is included under the aggregate of formations among the five aggregates—hence called equanimity-of-formations, to distinguish it from feeling-equanimity included under the aggregate of feeling. Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6, states that this mental activity has the three functions of evenness, uprightness, and effortlessness. It is a provisionally established dharma as a stage upon diligence and the three roots. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
