# Direct Explanation of the Treatise on the Hundred Dharmas

## Explanation（English）
A commentary on the Treatise on the Hundred Dharmas by the Ming-dynasty master Ǒuyì (Zhixu), giving a direct, line-by-line explanation of the treatise; it is one of the important commentaries for studying the hundred dharmas in Chinese Buddhism.
