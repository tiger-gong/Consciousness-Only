# three karmas of body, speech, and mind

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the fabricating activity of the three—body, speech, and mind—called the three karmas. (1) Bodily karma: karma issuing from all movements of the body, of two sorts—manifest bodily karma and unmanifest karma. (2) Verbal karma: karma issuing from the fabrications of the mouth. (3) Mental karma: karma issuing from the deliberation of the sixth consciousness, which is the function of the mental factor of volition (cetanā). As the Cheng weishi lun says of the factor of volition: “Its nature is to make the mind fabricate; its function is to direct the mind toward the wholesome and the like.” Hence mental karma is also called “volitional karma.” The intrinsic nature of volition is simply fabrication; thus by the power of its fabrication it corresponds with the consciousnesses and drives them to fabricate the wholesome, the unwholesome, the neutral, and so forth. The “consciousness” here refers to the sixth, mental, consciousness together with its concomitant mental factors.
