# maturation residual impressions

## Explanation（English）
See karmic seeds. Formed by the perfuming of contaminated wholesome and unwholesome karma, they can bring about the maturation fruit of the neutral kind, and are the indirect cause of the maturation transformer (the eighth consciousness).
