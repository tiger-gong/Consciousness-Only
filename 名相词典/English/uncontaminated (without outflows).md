# uncontaminated (without outflows)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*anāsrava*

## Explanation（English）
Uncontaminated dharmas free from afflictions, pure and undefiled—e.g., uncontaminated wisdom and the uncontaminated path. Capable of leaving saṃsāra.
