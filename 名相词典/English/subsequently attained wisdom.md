# subsequently attained wisdom
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pṛṣṭhalabdha-jñāna*

## Explanation（English）
Wisdom attained after non-discriminating wisdom; able to discriminate and cognize the illusory other-dependent dharmas and skillfully liberate beings; also called wisdom conforming to measure.
