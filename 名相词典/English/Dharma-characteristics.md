# Dharma-characteristics

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the essential characteristics (the marks of essence, 體相) possessed by all dharmas, or to their meaning-content (the marks of meaning, 義相). The distinctive feature of the Consciousness-Only (Yogācāra) school lies in analyzing and classifying the characteristics of dharmas; hence it is also called the Dharma-characteristics school (Faxiang zong). See the “Chapter on the Characteristics of All Dharmas” in the Saṃdhinirmocana-sūtra.
