# dissimulation (flattery)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*śāṭhya*

## Explanation（English）
Among the hundred dharmas in five categories of Yogācāra, a minor concomitant affliction. Dissimulation (flattery) is crooked flattery to win others' favor and achieve one's aims. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is dissimulation? In order to deceive others, falsely arranging manners, crooked cunning as nature; it obstructs non-dissimulation and has the activity of obstructing instruction.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
