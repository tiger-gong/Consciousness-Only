# Śīlabhadra
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Śīlabhadra*

## Explanation（English）
Skt. Śīlabhadra; eminent master of Nālandā, disciple of Dharmapāla; Xuanzang studied under him for five years, receiving the Yogācāra and Consciousness-Only teachings.
