# karmic force / karma
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*karma*

## Explanation（English）
“Karma” means fabrication or action; the “residual force” of such fabrication or action upon the future is called karmic force. Karma is further divided into wholesome and unwholesome; that wholesome and unwholesome karma have the power to give rise to fruits of pleasure and pain is itself proof of karmic force. All fruits of pleasure and pain are brought about by karmic force, hence the common saying, “the force of karma is inconceivable.”
