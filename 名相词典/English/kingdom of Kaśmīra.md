# kingdom of Kaśmīra
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Kaśmīra*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Kaśmīra, the name of an ancient country in northwest India—present-day Kashmir. A place where the Sarvāstivāda school flourished; the Mahāvibhāṣā was compiled there.
