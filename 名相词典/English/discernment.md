# discernment
> 校准自「wisdom (discernment)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*prajñā*

## Explanation（English）
One of the mental activities with specific objects among the mental activities. Wisdom is clear discernment. Cheng weishi lun says: “Toward the object contemplated, it has the nature of discriminating selection, and the function of cutting off doubt.” Discriminating selection is inferential knowledge: toward all knowable realms, selecting gain and loss and reasoning to a decision—hence selection is the self-nature of wisdom; by selection removing doubt is its function. In a deluded mind or a doubtful mind, wisdom cannot arise. Further, those of wrong view, because delusion is strong, cannot select and so wisdom does not arise.
