# vexation
> 校准自「spite (vexation)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pradāśa*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant passion among the mental activities. Spite is furious vexation: after anger and hostility, meeting adverse circumstances, one erupts in cruelty like a scorpion's sting. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is vexation? Preceded by anger and hostility, pursuing and contacting with fierce heat, ferocity as nature; it obstructs non-vexation and has the activity of stinging—for recalling past wrongs and meeting present adverse conditions, the mind becomes fierce, often uttering brutal coarse speech that stings others.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
