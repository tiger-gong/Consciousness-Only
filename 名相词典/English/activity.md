# activity
> 校准自「present activity (manifestation)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
Seeds are a doctrine established by the Dharma-characteristics school: latent capacities within the ālaya-consciousness are called seeds; when they function—i.e., when activity arises—they are no longer called seeds but consciousness. Activity is the functioning that can give rise to the distinct phenomena of form and mind. When latent seeds give rise to the dharmas of form and mind, this is called activity. Activity and seeds are mutually causal: seeds give rise to activity; activity perfumes seeds.
