# not blind to cause and effect

## Explanation（English）
The correct understanding in Baizhang's wild-fox kōan: even though one has awakened to the emptiness of no-self, one still deeply understands that cause and effect are not off by a hair and dare not treat them lightly—the counterpart of “not falling under cause and effect.”
