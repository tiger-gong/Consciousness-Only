# enlightenment
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*bodhi*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit bodhi; rendered by meaning as awakening, wisdom, knowing, or way. Broadly, it is the wisdom that severs the afflictions of the world and accomplishes nirvāṇa—the awakened wisdom that a Buddha, a pratyekabuddha, and a śrāvaka each attain at their respective fruit. Of these three kinds of bodhi, the Buddha's bodhi is supreme and ultimate, hence called anuttarā-samyak-saṃbodhi, translated as “unsurpassed perfect and complete awakening,” “unsurpassed right and universal wisdom,” “unsurpassed right and true way,” or “unsurpassed enlightenment.” Fascicle 4 of the Da zhidu lun says: “Bodhi is the name for the way of all Buddhas”; fascicle 44 says: “Bodhi, in Chinese, is unsurpassed wisdom.” This is the wisdom that clearly beholds the highest truth of dharma-nature—suchness.
