# dependent on others
> 校准自「other-dependent nature」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*paratantra-svabhāva*

## Explanation（English）
Other-dependent nature. Also called the dependent on others characteristic or conditioned self-nature; one of the three self-natures of Consciousness-Only. “Other” means condition as cause; a dharma that arises depending on condition as cause is called dependent on others. It belongs to conditioned dharmas; among the hundred dharmas, all but the six unconditioned—ninety-four in all—are included here. The manifold conditions are the four conditions; mind dharmas require all four to arise, while form dharmas need only the causal condition and the dominant condition. All conditioned phenomena arise by the combination of condition as cause and cease when they disperse—hence existent yet not existent, nonexistent yet not nonexistent: provisionally existent like an illusion. It may further be divided into defiled other-dependence (impure and mixed) and pure other-dependence (pure and pure); pure other-dependence may also be included under the perfected nature. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 8.
