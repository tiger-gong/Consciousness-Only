# Sautrāntika (the Sūtra school)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Sautrāntika*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Sautrāntika, the “Sūtra-authority School,” one of the sectarian schools. Taking only the scripture-basket (sūtra) as authoritative and not accepting the treatise-basket as the Buddha's word, it holds that “only the present is really existent, while past and future have no essence,” and often stands opposed to the Sarvāstivāda.
