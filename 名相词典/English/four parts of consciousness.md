# four parts of consciousness
> 校准自「four portions of the substance of consciousness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
The Consciousness-Only school holds that the cognitive function of mind-consciousness has a fourfold classification, arising when consciousness transforms: (1) the seen part—the object of cognition (the object-side), further divided into image-as-semblance and image-as-essence; (2) the seeing part—the subject of cognition, able to illuminate and know the seen part; (3) the self-authenticating part (substance part)—the function, on the substance itself, of verifying the seeing part; (4) the part that authenticates self-authentication—the cognitive function that verifies the self-authenticating part. All eight consciousnesses and their concomitant mental activities each possess these four parts. The seen part is the objective outer realm manifested by inner consciousness (not a really existent outer realm); the seeing part is the subjective cognitive function; the self-authenticating part is the substance of consciousness; the part that authenticates self-authentication is the further verification of the self-authenticating part.
