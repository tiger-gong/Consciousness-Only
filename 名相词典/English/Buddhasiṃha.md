# Buddhasiṃha
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Buddhasiṃha*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Buddhasiṃha, a fellow disciple (or disciple) of Asaṅga and Vasubandhu, one of the Yogācāra commentator-masters who propagated Consciousness-Only alongside such figures as Asvabhāva.
