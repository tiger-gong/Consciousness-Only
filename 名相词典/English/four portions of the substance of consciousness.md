# four portions of the substance of consciousness

## Explanation（English）
The Consciousness-Only school holds that the cognitive function of mind-consciousness has a fourfold classification, arising when consciousness transforms: (1) the image portion—the object of cognition (the object-side), further divided into image-as-semblance and image-as-essence; (2) the perceiving portion—the subject of cognition, able to illuminate and know the image portion; (3) the self-aware portion (substance portion)—the function, on the substance itself, of verifying the perceiving portion; (4) the re-aware portion—the cognitive function that verifies the self-aware portion. All eight consciousnesses and their concomitant mental factors each possess these four portions. The image portion is the objective outer realm manifested by inner consciousness (not a really existent outer realm); the perceiving portion is the subjective cognitive function; the self-aware portion is the substance of consciousness; the re-aware portion is the further verification of the self-aware portion.
