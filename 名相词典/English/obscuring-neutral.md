# obscuring-neutral
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*nivṛtāvyākṛta*

## Explanation（English）
Neither good nor bad (indeterminate), yet afflictions veil the true mind and obstruct the noble path—e.g., manas-consciousness and its concomitant afflictions.
