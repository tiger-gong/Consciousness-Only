# pure
> 校准自「uncontaminated (without outflows)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*anāsrava*

## Explanation（English）
Pure dharmas free from passions, pure and undefiled—e.g., pure wisdom and the pure path. Capable of leaving saṃsāra.
