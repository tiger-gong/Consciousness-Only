# spite (vexation)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pradāśa*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Spite is furious vexation: after anger and resentment, meeting adverse circumstances, one erupts in cruelty like a scorpion's sting. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is spite? Preceded by anger and resentment, pursuing and contacting with fierce heat, ferocity as nature; it obstructs non-spite and has the activity of stinging—for recalling past wrongs and meeting present adverse conditions, the mind becomes fierce, often uttering brutal coarse speech that stings others.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
