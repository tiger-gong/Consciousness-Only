# regret (remorse)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kaukṛtya*

## Explanation（English）
An indeterminate mental factor. Regret, also called kaukrtya (remorse over deeds): arising of a repentant mind toward what was previously done. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'Regret means kaukrtya: regarding deeds done as wrong, retrospective remorse as nature; it obstructs calm abiding as activity.' Remorse over past evil is wholesome; remorse over past good is unwholesome. Regret makes the mind uneasy and dejected, thus obstructing calm (and insight).
