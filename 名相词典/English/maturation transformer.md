# maturation transformer

## Explanation（English）
One of the three transformers in Consciousness-Only; the maturation transformer is the first transformer—that is, the transformation of the eighth, ālaya, consciousness. “Maturation” (vipāka; older translation “retribution”) is the general name for the fruit called forth by past wholesome and unwholesome karmic force; this matured fruit is the eighth consciousness itself. The maturation transformer is thus the ālaya's transforming.
