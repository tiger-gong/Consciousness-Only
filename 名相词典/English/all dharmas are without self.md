# all dharmas are without self

## Explanation（English）
The teaching that all dharmas, conditioned and unconditioned, are without an independent, permanent, unitary substantial self. It is one of the three Dharma-seals (“all dharmas are without self”) and is the guiding tenet of the Treatise on the Hundred Dharmas.
