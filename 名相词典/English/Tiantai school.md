# Tiantai school

## Explanation（English）
One of the eight Mahāyāna schools of China, founded by the Sui-dynasty master Zhiyi on the basis of the Lotus Sūtra. It classifies the teachings into the five periods and eight teachings and upholds the doctrines of “three thousand worlds in a single thought,” “the threefold contemplation in one mind,” and “nature-inclusion.”
