# volition
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*cetanā*

## Explanation（English）
One of the universally active mental factors among the mental factors. Volition is the will-function. Cheng weishi lun says: “It has the nature of causing the mind to act, and the function of employing the mind among the wholesome classes and so on.” Thus the self-nature of volition is only activity; by the force of that activity corresponding with the mind, it makes the mind, toward manifold good and evil objects, perform manifold good and evil karmic functions—this is the mental karma among the three karmas of body, speech, and mind. From the arising of mind-consciousness, from attention through to volition, good and evil thoughts are already formed and can no longer be stopped. From there to the object-specific [factors], it becomes the mind that must act.
