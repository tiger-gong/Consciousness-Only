# Venerable Śāriputra
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Śāriputra*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Śāriputra, one of the Buddha's ten great disciples, renowned as foremost in wisdom. He often expounded the Dharma on the Buddha's behalf and is the chief interlocutor in the questions and answers of many sūtras.
