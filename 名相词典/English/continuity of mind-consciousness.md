# continuity of mind-consciousness

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the continuous arising and ceasing of mind-consciousness, thought-moment after thought-moment without interruption. As the former thought ceases the next arises, arising in turn from the seeds in the ālaya-consciousness, constituting the continuity of mental life.
