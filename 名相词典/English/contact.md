# contact
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*sparśa*

## Explanation（English）
One of the universally active mental factors among the mental factors. Contact is the coming together of faculty, object, and consciousness—hence also called the “threefold combination.” Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 3, says: “Contact means the discriminative alteration of the threefold combination; it has the nature of making mind and mental factors contact the object, and the function of being the basis on which feeling, perception, volition, and so on depend. Faculty, object, and consciousness mutually accord—hence named threefold combination.” The treatise means: when faculty and object engage, consciousness necessarily arises with them; the faculty is that on which consciousness depends, the object that which consciousness takes; when the three combine, mind and mental factors can share one object, and thereby feeling, perception, and volition arise. At the stage of the threefold combination of faculty, object, and consciousness there is the capacity to give rise in accord to all mental factors—this is called alteration. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
