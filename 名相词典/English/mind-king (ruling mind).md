# mind-king (ruling mind)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*citta-rāja*

## Explanation（English）
Also called the eight consciousness mind-kings: the eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mental, manas, and ālaya consciousnesses. The mind-king is the subject of cognition; mental factors are the psychological functions that arise accompanying the mind-king.
