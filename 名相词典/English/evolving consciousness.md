# evolving consciousness

## Explanation（English）
The Consciousness-Only school takes the first seven consciousnesses as the seven evolving consciousnesses. “Evolving” means transforming and arising: the first seven—eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, mental, and manas—depend on the ālaya (storehouse-consciousness), arise by taking form, sound, smell, taste, touch, and dharmas as objects, can alter the three feelings of suffering, pleasure, and equanimity, and transform among the three natures of good, evil, and neutral—hence seven evolving consciousnesses. Further, relative to the seventh, the first six are called the six evolving consciousnesses.
