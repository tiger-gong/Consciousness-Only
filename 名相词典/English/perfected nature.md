# perfected nature
> 校准自「perfectly accomplished nature」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pariniṣpanna-svabhāva*

## Explanation（English）
Also called the perfected characteristic; one of the three self-natures of Consciousness-Only. It means that true suchness has three qualities—perfect, accomplished, and real: perfect, in that the wondrous principle of true suchness pervades everywhere; accomplished, in that the true substance of true suchness abides constantly without arising or ceasing; real, in that the nature of true suchness abides constantly and pervades thoroughly. The perfected nature is the truth revealed by emptying the two obstacles of passion and of the knowable—that is, the true essential nature of all dharmas; it is also named true suchness, true characteristic, Dharma-realm, and Dharma-nature. The true nature of all dharmas revealed by the emptiness of self and the emptiness of dharmas is the perfected nature. Upon the dependent on others, constantly free from the imagined nature—that is the perfected nature.
