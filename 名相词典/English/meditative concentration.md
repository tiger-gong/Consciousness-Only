# meditative concentration
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*dhyāna*

## Explanation（English）
A combined term for the Sanskrit dhyāna and samādhi, referring to the cultivation of quieting distraction, fixing the mind on a single object, and making the mind focused and still; it is one of the three trainings and one of the six perfections.
