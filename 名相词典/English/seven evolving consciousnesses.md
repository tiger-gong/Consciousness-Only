# seven evolving consciousnesses

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the first seven consciousnesses; there are two explanations. (1) Taking the eighth as the root consciousness, the first seven are the consciousnesses that arise and evolve from it—hence “seven evolving consciousnesses.” (2) On the path of cultivation through the ten stages (bhūmis), these first seven, when one enters contemplation, are of uncontaminated wholesome nature, yet on emerging from contemplation they are again of contaminated defiled nature; because of this turning and change, they are called seven evolving consciousnesses. The eighth, after the diamond-like mind, turns from contaminated to uncontaminated, transforming consciousness into wisdom: that turn is once and for all, so it is not called an evolving consciousness.
