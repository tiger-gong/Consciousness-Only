# immediate-antecedent condition
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*samanantara-pratyaya*

## Explanation（English）
When the preceding moment of mind and mental factors ceases, the condition that equally and without interval opens the way for the arising of the succeeding moment of mind and mental factors.
