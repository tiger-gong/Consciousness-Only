# faith
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*śraddhā*

## Explanation（English）
One of the wholesome mental factors among the mental factors. Faith is firm conviction in the Buddhist teachings. Cheng weishi lun says: “What is faith? Toward reality, virtue, and capacity, deeply enduring, delighting, and desiring, with purity of mind as nature; counteracting non-faith and delighting in the good as function.” Faith has three kinds: first, deep faith without doubt in the real facts and real principles of dharmas; second, deep faith and delight in the pure virtues of the Triple Gem; third, deep faith that all wholesome dharmas have power and can be accomplished. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
