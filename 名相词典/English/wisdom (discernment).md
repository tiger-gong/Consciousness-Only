# wisdom (discernment)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*prajñā*

## Explanation（English）
One of the object-specific mental factors among the mental factors. Wisdom is clear discernment. Cheng weishi lun says: “Toward the object contemplated, it has the nature of discriminating selection, and the function of cutting off doubt.” Discriminating selection is inferential knowledge: toward all knowable realms, selecting gain and loss and reasoning to a decision—hence selection is the self-nature of wisdom; by selection removing doubt is its function. In a deluded mind or a doubtful mind, wisdom cannot arise. Further, those of wrong view, because delusion is strong, cannot select and so wisdom does not arise.
