# contaminated seeds

## Explanation（English）
“Contaminated” means involving afflictions and defilement. Contaminated seeds are the capacity to give rise to contaminated dharmas, contrasted with uncontaminated seeds. The innate and newly perfumed contaminated seeds in beings' ālaya-consciousness, when they meet conditions, give rise to defiled present activity.
