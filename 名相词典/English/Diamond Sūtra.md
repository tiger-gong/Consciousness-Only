# Diamond Sūtra
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Vajracchedikā Prajñāpāramitā*

## Explanation（English）
Fully titled the Vajracchedikā-prajñāpāramitā-sūtra (Diamond Sūtra), translated by Kumārajīva; in one fascicle. A key text of the Prajñāpāramitā corpus, it teaches the emptiness of all dharmas, giving rise to the mind without abiding anywhere, and the breaking of the two graspings at self and dharmas; it is very widely circulated in China.
