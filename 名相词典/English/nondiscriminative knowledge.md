# nondiscriminative knowledge
> 校准自「non-discriminating wisdom」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*nirvikalpajñāna*

## Explanation（English）
Non-discriminating wisdom that directly realizes true suchness, free from subject–object discrimination—i.e., fundamental wisdom. Arises at the bodhisattva's path of seeing; able to cut delusion and realize the principle.
