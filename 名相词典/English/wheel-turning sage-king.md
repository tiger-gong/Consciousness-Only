# wheel-turning sage-king
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*cakravartin*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit cakravartin, the ideal monarch who turns the wheel-treasure and governs the world by the true Dharma. Possessing the seven treasures and four virtues, he rules the four quarters not by military might but by the transforming power of virtue.
