# four conditions
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*catuḥ-pratyaya*

## Explanation（English）
Cause-as-condition, equal-and-immediate condition, object-as-condition, and dominant condition. The four conditions on which all conditioned dharmas arise; mental dharmas require all four, while material dharmas need only cause-as-condition and dominant condition.
