# faith, vow, and holding the name (nianfo)

## Explanation（English）
The essential framework of Pure Land practice: deep faith (faith), earnest vow to be reborn (vow), and firmly holding the name of Amitābha Buddha (holding the name); when these three are complete, one attains rebirth.
