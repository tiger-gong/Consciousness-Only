# repentance

## Explanation（English）
A compound of a Sanskrit transliteration and a Chinese word: “confession” means to disclose one's former faults, and “repentance” means to change the past and cultivate for the future. It refers to disclosing the evil karma one has created, arousing shame, and vowing not to repeat it, so as to purify away karmic hindrances.
