# laziness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kausīdya*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Laziness is the opposite of diligence: neither cultivating the good nor cutting off the evil—indeed, even zealous toward unwholesome deeds. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is laziness? In the matter of cultivating wholesome and abandoning unwholesome classes, indolence as nature; it obstructs diligence and has the activity of increasing defilement.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
