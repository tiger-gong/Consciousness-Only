# indolence
> 校准自「laziness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kausīdya*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant passion among the mental activities. Laziness is the opposite of diligence: neither cultivating the good nor cutting off the evil—indeed, even zealous toward unwholesome deeds. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is indolence? In the matter of cultivating wholesome and abandoning unwholesome classes, indolence as nature; it obstructs diligence and has the activity of increasing defilement.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
