# Master Hongyi

## Explanation（English）
The modern eminent monk Li Shutong (1880–1942). After going forth he devoted himself to propagating the Nanshan Vinaya, keeping the precepts with great strictness; he was a representative figure in the revival of Vinaya studies in Republican-era Buddhism.
