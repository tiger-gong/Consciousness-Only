# dissimulation
> 校准自「concealment (of faults)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*mrakṣa*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant passion among the mental activities. Concealment is covering over: having committed a fault, fearing loss of reputation or gain, one hides the wrongdoing. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is concealment? Toward faults, hiding as nature—toward one's own evils, fearing loss of profit and fame, concealment as nature; it obstructs non-concealment and has the activity of remorseful distress—for one who conceals sins must later suffer regret.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
