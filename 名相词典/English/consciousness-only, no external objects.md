# consciousness-only, no [external] objects

## Explanation（English）
Core thesis of the Consciousness-Only school: external objects are not truly existent apart from consciousness; all objects are image-aspects transformed by inner consciousness—hence “objects not apart from consciousness,” “consciousness-only, no [external] objects.” It does not deny phenomena, but denies real dharmas outside the mind.
