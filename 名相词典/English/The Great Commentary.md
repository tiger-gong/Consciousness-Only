# The Great Commentary
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Mahāvibhāṣā*

## Explanation（English）
Fully titled the Abhidharma-mahāvibhāṣā-śāstra, translated by Xuanzang; in two hundred fascicles. The magnum opus of the Sarvāstivāda school, an extensive commentary on the Jñānaprasthāna, it is a treasury of the doctrines of sectarian (Abhidharma) Buddhism.
