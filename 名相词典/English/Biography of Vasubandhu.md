# Biography of Vasubandhu

## Explanation（English）
Translated by Paramārtha in the Chen dynasty; in one fascicle. A biography recording the life and deeds of the bodhisattva Vasubandhu (Sanskrit Vasubandhu, transliterated Posoupandou) and his elder brother Asaṅga; an important biographical source for studying the Consciousness-Only patriarchs.
