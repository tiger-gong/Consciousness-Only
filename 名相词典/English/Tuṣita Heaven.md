# Tuṣita Heaven
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Tuṣita*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Tuṣita, rendered “the Heaven of Contentment,” the fourth of the six heavens of the desire realm. Its inner court is where the bodhisattva of one-more-birth expounds the Dharma; the bodhisattva Maitreya now dwells there.
