# image portion (seen portion)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*nimitta-bhāga*

## Explanation（English）
One of the four portions of mind dharmas: the objective appearance manifested on the mind’s own substance as what the perceiving portion cognizes, encompassing all so-called objective phenomena. When mind-consciousness arises it manifests the image and perceiving portions: the perceiving portion is that which cognizes, the image portion the cognized objective appearance. The image portion is the objective appearance manifested by the form-dharma seeds of the eighth consciousness; the semblance-image is not the essential form of an outer object, but, relying on the image-portion form of the eighth, a further semblance is re-manifested on the eye-consciousness, which the perceiving portion of the eye-consciousness then cognizes. Hence it is said: “What consciousness cognizes is only what consciousness transforms.”
