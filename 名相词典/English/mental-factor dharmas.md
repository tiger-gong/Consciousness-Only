# mental-factor dharmas
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*caitta*

## Explanation（English）
Abbreviated as mental factors: the psychological functions subordinate to the mind-king and arising in correspondence with it. Among the Five Categories and Hundred Dharmas, fifty-one are established, in six divisions: universally active, object-specific, wholesome, afflictive, secondary afflictive, and indeterminate.
