# name-and-word seeds

## Explanation（English）
Also called homogeneous-outflow residual impressions: the seeds that are the direct causal condition producing all dharmas. They grow through the perfuming of the three natures—wholesome, unwholesome, and neutral—by the first seven consciousnesses; they can induce fruits of the homogeneous-outflow class that share the same nature, as when a wholesome cause yields a wholesome fruit and an unwholesome cause an unwholesome fruit. Seeds perfumed by expressive name-and-word (verbal expression) and by object-manifesting name-and-word (consciousness cognizing objects in a speech-like manner of discernment) both belong to name-and-word seeds.
