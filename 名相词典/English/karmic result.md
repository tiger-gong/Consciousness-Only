# karmic result
> 校准自「retribution」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

## Explanation（English）
The result called forth by past karmic causes. Also termed retribution, ripening of fruit, retributive fruit, or result of retribution (vipāka). It is of two kinds: (1) General retribution—the fruit that comes from the “impelling karma” (the karma of general retribution); for example, a person's very existence comes from the impelling karma of a former life. (2) Particular retribution—each person's individual fruit, coming from the “completing karma” (the karma of particular retribution), also called the completing fruit; thus even those born alike as humans differ as male or female, beautiful or ugly, poor or rich—this is particular retribution. In terms of time, there are the three periods of karma with their three periods of retribution: (1) retribution ripening in the present—karma made in this life reaps its fruit in this life (also called present retribution); (2) retribution ripening in the next birth—karma made in this life reaps its fruit in the next life; (3) retribution ripening in a later life—karma made in this life reaps its fruit in a still later life. Among the six destinies, the human and heavenly destinies are the fruit obtained by keeping the five precepts and practicing the ten wholesome deeds, hence called wholesome fruit; yet this wholesome fruit still involves delusion and passion, so it is also called the “topsy-turvy wholesome fruit.”
