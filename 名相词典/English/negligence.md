# negligence
> 校准自「laxity (heedlessness)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pramāda*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant passion among the mental activities. Laxity is dissolute negligence: unable to guard against defiled dharmas, unwilling to cultivate pure ones. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is negligence? Toward defiled and pure classes, unable to prevent or cultivate, indulgence as nature; it obstructs non-negligence and has the activity of serving as the basis for increasing evil and diminishing good.'
