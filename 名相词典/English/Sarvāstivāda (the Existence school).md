# Sarvāstivāda (the Existence school)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Sarvāstivāda / Mūlasarvāstivāda*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Sarvāstivāda, the “School that Asserts the Existence of All,” the most influential school of sectarian Buddhism. It maintains that “the three times are really existent and the essence of dharmas is eternally existent,” and establishes the Five Categories and Seventy-five Dharmas.
