# self means sovereign controller

## Explanation（English）
An explanation of the word “self”: “self” has the sense of “sovereign controller”—the notion that there is a permanent, unitary substance that can freely govern body and mind. The Buddha-Dharma, however, teaches that this “self” cannot be found, and so sets forth “no-self.”
