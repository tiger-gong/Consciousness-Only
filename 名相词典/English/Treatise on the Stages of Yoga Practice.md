# Treatise on the Stages of Yoga Practice
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra*

## Explanation（English）
Expounded by the bodhisattva Maitreya and recorded by Asaṅga, translated by Xuanzang; in one hundred fascicles. The foundational treatise of the Yogācāra school, it expounds at length the objects, practices, and fruits of the three vehicles, structured especially around the “seventeen stages,” whence its name.
