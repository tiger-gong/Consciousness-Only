# two-portion school

## Explanation（English）
The treatise-master Nanda advocated a two-portion theory of mind-consciousness: the perceiving portion and the image portion. There are also Sthiramati’s one-portion theory, Dignāga’s three-portion theory, and Dharmapāla’s four-portion theory. See “four portions of the substance of consciousness.”
