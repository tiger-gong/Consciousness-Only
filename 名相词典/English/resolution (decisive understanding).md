# resolution (decisive understanding)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*adhimokṣa*

## Explanation（English）
One of the object-specific mental factors among the mental factors. “Superior” means outstanding; “understanding” means view—hence an outstanding understanding. Cheng weishi lun says: “Toward a determined object, it has the nature of sealing and holding, and the function of being unswayable.” A determined object means, toward a real object engaged or a doctrinal object, there is no hesitation: for example, engaging blue and reckoning it blue without doubting it red or indigo; or, influenced by a certain teaching, accepting its meaning without doubt—all are called determined objects. When mind-consciousness cognizes a determined object, there is the function of examining, deciding, and sealing-holding—this is the self-nature of resolution. Once examined, decided, and sealed, it cannot be swayed—that is its function. Toward a hesitant object, resolution does not arise.
