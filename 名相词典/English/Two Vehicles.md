# Two Vehicles

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the two vehicles of the śrāvakas and the pratyekabuddhas, both chiefly aiming at delivering oneself and escaping birth-and-death; together they belong to the Lesser Vehicle, set in contrast to the Bodhisattva Vehicle (Great Vehicle).
