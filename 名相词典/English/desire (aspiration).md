# desire (aspiration)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*chanda*

## Explanation（English）
Also called joyful desire: the mental function of aspiration and desire, hoping for the task to be done. The Consciousness-Only school takes it as an object-specific mental factor arising toward an object of wish. Desire has the three natures of good, evil, and neutral. Wholesome desire is the basis that gives rise to a diligent mind; among unwholesome desires, craving another’s wealth is greed, one of the root afflictions. Desire has many kinds, such as the five desires, six desires, and three desires.
