# that which is imagined

## Explanation（English）
The counterpart of that which imagines. When the delusively discriminating mind-consciousness thoroughly imagines and calculates its cognitive objects, discriminating and clinging to them as a real self and real dharmas, those things thus discriminated are that which is imagined. It is not that there are real objects outside consciousness; rather, the imagining mind mistakenly takes the illusory-provisional as real.
