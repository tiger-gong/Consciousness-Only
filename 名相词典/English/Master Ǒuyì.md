# Master Ǒuyì

## Explanation（English）
One of the four eminent monks of the late Ming, named Zhixu (1599–1655). Harmonizing the Tiantai, Chan, Vinaya, and Pure Land traditions, he was a prolific author; among his works is the Direct Explanation of the Treatise on the Hundred Dharmas, making him an important commentator for the study of the hundred dharmas in Chinese Buddhism.
