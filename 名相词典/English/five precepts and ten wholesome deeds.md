# five precepts and ten wholesome deeds

## Explanation（English）
The five precepts—not killing, not stealing, not engaging in sexual misconduct, not lying, and not drinking intoxicants—are the fundamental precepts for lay disciples. The ten wholesome deeds are the ten good karmas—three of body, four of speech, and three of mind. Practicing the five precepts and ten wholesome deeds yields the wholesome fruit of the human and heavenly destinies.
