# hindrance of afflictions
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kleśāvaraṇa*

## Explanation（English）
The afflictions rooted in grasping at a self, which obstruct nirvāṇa and screen beings from liberation, are called the hindrance of afflictions. Both the two vehicles and bodhisattvas must sever it.
