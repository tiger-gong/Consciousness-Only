# auditory consciousness
> 校准自「ear-consciousness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*śrotra-vijñāna*

## Explanation（English）
Discriminating consciousness that arises based on the ear faculty, taking sound as its object.
