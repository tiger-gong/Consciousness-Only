# seeing part
> 校准自「perceiving portion (seeing portion)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*darśana-bhāga*

## Explanation（English）
One of the four parts of consciousness. It refers to the cognizing function of the consciousnesses—the subject that knows things, able to illuminate and know the cognitive object (the seen part). “Perceiving” means illuminating-seeing, the mind’s nature being clear. When mind-consciousness arises, from its own substance it manifests the image and seeing parts: the seen part is form dharma, the seeing part is mind dharma; both are manifested by the substance of consciousness, gathering objects back into mind—hence Consciousness-Only is established.
