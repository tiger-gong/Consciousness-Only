# Treatise on the Establishment of Consciousness-Only
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi*

## Explanation（English）
Compiled and translated by Xuanzang from the ten great masters' commentaries on the Thirty Verses on Consciousness-Only; ten fascicles; fundamental treatise of the Faxiang Consciousness-Only school, taking Dharmapāla's view as the orthodox one.
