# shamelessness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*āhrīkya*

## Explanation（English）
A medium concomitant affliction among the mental factors. Shamelessness is the opposite of shame: lacking a sense of moral shame, disregarding one's own dignity, rejecting the teaching of the wise, and not accepting worldly or transmundane wholesome dharmas. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is shamelessness? Disregarding one's own Dharma, lightly rejecting the worthy and good as nature; it obstructs shame and has the activity of growing evil conduct.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
