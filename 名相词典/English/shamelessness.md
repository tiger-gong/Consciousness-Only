# shamelessness
> 校准自「non-embarrassment」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*anapatrāpya*

## Explanation（English）
A medium concomitant passion among the mental activities. Non-embarrassment is the opposite of embarrassment: without a sense of propriety, disregarding worldly censure, unafraid of public opinion. The Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6, says: 'What is shamelessness? Disregarding the world, esteeming the violent and evil as nature; it obstructs embarrassment and has the activity of growing evil conduct.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
