# re-aware portion (awareness of self-awareness)

## Explanation（English）
The fourth of the four portions of the substance of consciousness: further verifying the self-aware portion. The self-aware portion verifies the perceiving portion; the re-aware portion verifies the self-aware portion; and the re-aware portion is in turn verified by the self-aware portion—the two mutually cognize and verify each other, so there is no need to posit a fifth portion.
