# four-portion school

## Explanation（English）
The doctrine of the four portions of the substance of consciousness is regarded as the Consciousness-Only orthodox view. Among the Ten Great Treatise-masters: Sthiramati posited only the self-aware portion (one-portion school); Nanda posited the perceiving and image portions (two-portion school); Dignāga posited perceiving, image, and self-aware portions (three-portion school); Dharmapāla posited perceiving, image, self-aware, and re-aware portions (four-portion school). In perfuming, the perceiving portion perfumes cognizing seeds, the image portion perfumes cognized seeds, and the substance portion also perfumes; the re-aware portion has no perfuming efficacy. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 2.
