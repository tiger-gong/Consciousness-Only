# selflessness of persons
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pudgala-nairātmya*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit pudgala-nairātmya, one of the two selflessnesses. It teaches that a sentient being's life is merely the provisional assemblage of the five aggregates, with no permanent, unitary, controlling substantial self; the counterpart of the “selflessness of dharmas.”
