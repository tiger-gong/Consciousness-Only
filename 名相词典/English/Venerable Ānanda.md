# Venerable Ānanda
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Ānanda*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Ānanda, the Buddha's cousin, who attended upon the Buddha for twenty-five years and was foremost in hearing (learning). After the Buddha's parinirvāṇa he recited the scripture-basket at the council; the “Thus have I heard” that opens the sūtras issued from his mouth.
