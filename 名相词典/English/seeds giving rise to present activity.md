# seeds giving rise to present activity

## Explanation（English）
When seeds in the ālaya-consciousness meet conditions and give rise to the present-activity fruit of the dharmas of form and mind, this is called seeds giving rise to present activity. Seeds are the cause, present activity the effect; at the same time present activity can perfume new seeds stored back in the ālaya-consciousness, forming the continuous mutual succession of seeds and present activity.
