# Ornament of the Mahāyāna Sūtras
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāra*

## Explanation（English）
Composed by Asaṅga, translated by Prabhākaramitra in the Tang dynasty; in thirteen fascicles. Its content expounds the bodhisattva's arousing of the aspiration, his practice, and the various methods he should cultivate. Also called the Treatise on the Ornament of the Mahāyāna; the whole work is divided into twenty-four chapters. According to fascicle 1 of the Lamp of the Definitive Meaning of the Cheng weishi lun and fascicle 4 of the Commentary on the Cheng weishi lun, the root verses were composed by the bodhisattva Maitreya, while the prose commentary was composed by the bodhisattva Vasubandhu.
