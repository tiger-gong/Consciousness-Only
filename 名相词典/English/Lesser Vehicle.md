# Lesser Vehicle
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Hīnayāna*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Hīnayāna, meaning “the lesser vehicle.” It refers to the teachings of the two vehicles—śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas—whose goal is one's own liberation, realizing arhatship or pratyekabuddhahood; set in contrast to the Great Vehicle.
