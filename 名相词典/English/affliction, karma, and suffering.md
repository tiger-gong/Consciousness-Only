# affliction, karma, and suffering
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kleśa, karma, duḥkha*

## Explanation（English）
Refers to the three—affliction (delusion), karma (fabrication), and suffering (the painful fruit)—which arise from one another in turn as the process of transmigration: from delusion one creates karma, from karma one calls forth suffering, and within suffering delusion again arises, cycling without end.
