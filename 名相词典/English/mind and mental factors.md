# mind and mental factors
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*citta and caitta*

## Explanation（English）
A joint term for mind dharmas (the eight ruling minds) and mental-factor dharmas (the fifty-one mental factors). The mind-king is the subject of cognition, and the mental factors are the various psychological functions that arise in company with the mind-king.
