# mind-consciousness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*citta-vijñāna*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit citta-vijñāna, broadly referring to the mental subject that discerns, deliberates, and accumulates—that is, a general term for the eight consciousnesses; it is the central concern of Consciousness-Only studies.
