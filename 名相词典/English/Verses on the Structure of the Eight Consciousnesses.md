# Verses on the Structure of the Eight Consciousnesses

## Explanation（English）
Comprising four sections, twelve verses, forty-eight lines. Composed by the Tang-dynasty Tripiṭaka-master Xuanzang. It summarizes the Consciousness-Only teaching, focusing on the question of mind-and-consciousness that lies at its heart. The first three verses treat the first five consciousnesses; verses four to six the sixth consciousness; verses seven to nine the seventh consciousness; the last three verses the eighth consciousness. Commentaries are numerous, the more famous being Putai's Supplementary Notes on the Verses (Ming), Mingyu's Correct Meaning of the Supplementary Notes, Zhixu's Direct Explanation of the Verses, and Guangyi's Compiled Explanation of the Verses.
