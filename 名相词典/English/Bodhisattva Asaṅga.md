# Bodhisattva Asaṅga
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*Asaṅga*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit Asaṅga, the elder brother of Vasubandhu, born about nine hundred years after the Buddha's parinirvāṇa. Transmitting the teaching of Maitreya, he recorded the Yogācārabhūmi-śāstra and composed the Mahāyāna-saṃgraha and others; he was one of the founders of the Indian Yogācāra school.
