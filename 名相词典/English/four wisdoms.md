# four wisdoms
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*jñāna*

## Explanation（English）
The four uncontaminated wisdoms formed by transforming the eight consciousnesses: transforming the first five yields the Wisdom of Accomplishing What Is to Be Done; the sixth, the Wisdom of Wondrous Observation; the seventh, the Wisdom of Equality; and the eighth, the Great Perfect Mirror Wisdom.
