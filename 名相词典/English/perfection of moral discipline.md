# perfection of moral discipline
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*śīla-pāramitā*

## Explanation（English）
It signifies conduct, habit, character, morality, and reverent devotion. It is one of the three trainings, one of the six perfections, and one of the ten perfections. In cultivating the Consciousness-Only practice and entering the path of cultivation, one practices the ten excellent practices, which are the ten perfections; the second of these is the perfection of moral discipline. Discipline (śīla) is of three kinds: (1) the discipline of restraining regulations—strictly keeping the precepts and rules so as to prevent transgression; (2) the discipline of embracing wholesome dharmas—upholding all the wholesome dharmas that should be cultivated and realized; (3) the discipline of benefiting sentient beings—relieving beings' sufferings and bringing them benefit and joy. See the entry “ten perfections.”
