**§2**

**中文**
因此龙树菩萨说：「以有空义故，一切法得成。」因为一切法「[[名相词典/中文/毕竟空|毕竟空]]」，所以一切因果原理，才能够建立起来。
换句话说：既然「一切法」体性是[[名相词典/中文/毕竟空|毕竟空]]，表示我们的生命是可以改造、表示佛道可以成就。这是我们在学习「色、心」二法之前，一定要清楚的观念。你现在的身心状况，透过你[[名相词典/中文/心识|心识]]的造作，都是可以改变的！

**English**
Therefore Bodhisattva Nāgārjuna said: “Because there is the meaning of [[名相词典/English/emptiness|emptiness]], all dharmas can be established.” Precisely because all dharmas are “ultimately empty,” the principles of cause and effect can be set up.
In other words: since the nature of “all dharmas” is ultimately empty, this shows that our life can be remade, and that the Buddha Path can be accomplished. This is a view we must be clear about before we study the two dharmas of “form and mind.” Your present condition of body and mind, through the activity of your [[名相词典/English/mind-consciousness|mind-consciousness]], can all be changed!

**Français**
C’est pourquoi le bodhisattva Nāgārjuna a dit : « Parce qu’il y a le sens du vide, tous les dharmas peuvent s’établir. » Justement parce que tous les dharmas sont « ultimement vides », les principes de cause et d’effet peuvent être établis.
Autrement dit : puisque la nature de « tous les dharmas » est ultimement vide, cela montre que notre vie peut être transformée, et que la Voie du Bouddha peut être accomplie. C’est une conception qu’il faut clairement comprendre avant d’étudier les deux dharmas de « forme et esprit ». Votre condition présente du corps et de l’esprit, par l’activité de votre [[名相词典/Français/conscience mentale|conscience mentale]], peut entièrement être changée !

**Tiếng Việt**
Vì vậy Bồ-tát Long [[名相词典/TiếngViệt/thọ|Thọ]] nói: 「Do có nghĩa không, nên tất cả pháp được thành.」 Vì tất cả pháp 「rốt ráo không」, nên mọi nguyên lý nhân quả mới có thể được kiến lập.
Nói cách khác: đã 「tất cả pháp」 thể tánh là rốt ráo không, tức là mạng sống của chúng ta có thể cải tạo, Phật đạo có thể thành tựu. **§14**

**中文**
『前之四位，收世出世有为诸法。第五无为，收世出世[[名相词典/中文/无为法|无为法]]性。』前面四个，包括「[[名相词典/中文/心法|心法]]、[[名相词典/中文/心所有法|心所有法]]、[[名相词典/中文/色法|色法]]、心不相应行法」，都是属于有为、有造作、有活动的。第五个「无为」，是属于[[名相词典/中文/无为法|无为法]]；它是一种寂静、没有造作的状态。譬如虚空，我们看虚空里面一下子造一间房子，地震一下子就把房子给破坏掉；一下子种了一棵树，一下子就把这棵树砍掉了；树跟房子都是变化的，但是「虚空」不变化，不管多大的地震，虚空没有任何的变化。你不要以为虚空就是空气，空气会流动，虚空不是一种动相，它空的相貌是没有造作[[名相词典/中文/因缘|因缘]]的，这就是「[[名相词典/中文/有为法|有为法]]」跟「[[名相词典/中文/无为法|无为法]]」的差别；「[[名相词典/中文/有为法|有为法]]」是[[名相词典/中文/因缘|因缘]]造作之法，都是造作而生起的，也会因造作而灭掉。另外是[[名相词典/中文/无为法|无为法]]。这是把「[[名相词典/中文/五位百法|五位百法]]」分成两类，以下详细说明。

**English**
“The first four categories gather all [[名相词典/English/conditioned dharmas|conditioned dharmas]] of the world and beyond the world. The fifth, the unconditioned, gathers the unconditioned dharma-nature of the world and beyond the world.” The first four—including “[[名相词典/English/mind dharmas|mind dharmas]], [[名相词典/English/mental factors|mental factors]], [[名相词典/English/form dharmas|form dharmas]], and formations dissociated from mind”—all belong to the conditioned: they have fabrication and activity. The fifth, the “unconditioned,” belongs to [[名相词典/English/unconditioned dharmas|unconditioned dharmas]]; it is a state of stillness without fabrication. Take empty space as an example: within empty space someone suddenly builds a house, and an earthquake suddenly destroys the house; suddenly a tree is planted, and suddenly the tree is cut down. The tree and the house both change, but “empty space” does not change—no matter how great the earthquake, empty space undergoes no change at all. Do not think empty space is air; air flows. Empty space is not a moving appearance; the mark of its [[名相词典/English/emptiness|emptiness]] is that it has no fabricated [[名相词典/English/causes and conditions|causes and conditions]].
