# mental activities
> 校准自「mental factors」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*caitta*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit caitta, fully “dharmas that are mental activities,” referring to the psychological functions that are subordinate to and arise in correspondence with the mind-king. The Five Categories and Hundred Dharmas establish fifty-one of them, in six divisions: universal, with specific objects, wholesome, of the passions, secondary of the passions, and indeterminate.
