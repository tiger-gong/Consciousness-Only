# retribution
> 校准自「maturation (ripened result)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vipāka*

## Explanation（English）
Retribution of a different kind: the cause may be good or bad, the result only indeterminate; or ripening at a different time, or through transformation. Refers to the indeterminate retribution induced by karmic causes.
