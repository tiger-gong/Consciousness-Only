# mind and mental activities
> 校准自「mind and mental factors」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*citta and caitta*

## Explanation（English）
A joint term for mind dharmas (the eight ruling minds) and dharmas that are mental activities (the fifty-one mental activities). The mind-king is the subject of cognition, and the mental activities are the various psychological functions that arise in company with the mind-king.
