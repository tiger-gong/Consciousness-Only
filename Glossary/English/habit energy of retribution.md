# habit energy of retribution
> 校准自「maturation residual impressions」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
See karmic seeds. Formed by the perfuming of impure wholesome and unwholesome karma, they can bring about the retribution fruit of the neutral kind, and are the indirect cause of the retribution transformer (the eighth consciousness).
