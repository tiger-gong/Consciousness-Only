# vigor
> 校准自「diligence (vigor)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vīrya*

## Explanation（English）
One of the eleven good mental activities among the mental activities. Also called diligence: assiduous, tireless effort in cultivating the path and doing good. Cheng weishi lun says: “Diligence is energy: in the tasks of cultivating the wholesome classes and abandoning the unwholesome, it has courage and vigor as nature; counteracting indolence and completing the good as function.” “Pure” means unmixed; “advancing” means not retreating; courageous without sloth, vigorous without fear. Diligence is cultivating good and cutting off evil; one who is assiduous in evil is precisely inverted and is not called diligence. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
