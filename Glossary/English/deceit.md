# deceit
> 校准自「deception」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*māyā*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant passion among the mental activities. Deception is fraudulently claiming virtue one lacks in order to obtain fame or profit. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is deceit? For the sake of gain and renown, falsely displaying virtue, craftiness as nature; it obstructs non-deceit and has the activity of wrong livelihood.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
