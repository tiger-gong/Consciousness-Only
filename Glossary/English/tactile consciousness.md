# tactile consciousness
> 校准自「body-consciousness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kāya-vijñāna*

## Explanation（English）
Discriminating consciousness that arises based on the body faculty, taking tangibles as its object.
