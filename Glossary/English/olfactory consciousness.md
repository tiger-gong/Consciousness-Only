# olfactory consciousness
> 校准自「nose-consciousness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*ghrāṇa-vijñāna*

## Explanation（English）
Discriminating consciousness that arises based on the nose faculty, taking odor as its object.
