# good mental activities
> 校准自「wholesome mental factors」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
Faith, diligence, shame, embarrassment, noncraving, non-hatred, non-delusion, serenity, non-negligence, equanimity, and harmlessness—eleven that correspond only with wholesome minds.
