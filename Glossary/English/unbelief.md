# unbelief
> 校准自「lack of faith」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*āśraddhya*

## Explanation（English）
Among the hundred dharmas in five categories of Yogācāra, a major concomitant passion. Lack of faith means absence of true confidence: without genuine faith, one cannot affirm all real facts and principles. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is unbelief? Toward reality, virtue, and capacity, not enduring delight and aspiration, mental foulness as nature; it obstructs pure faith and has the activity of serving as a basis of downfall—for those lacking faith are often lazy.'
