# subsequently acquired knowledge
> 校准自「subsequently attained wisdom」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pṛṣṭhalabdha-jñāna*

## Explanation（English）
Wisdom attained after nondiscriminative knowledge; able to discriminate and cognize the illusory dependent on others dharmas and skillfully liberate beings; also called wisdom conforming to measure.
