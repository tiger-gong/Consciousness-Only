# six groups of mental activities
> 校准自「six divisions of mental factors」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
The six classifications of mental activities: universal (five), with specific objects (five), wholesome (eleven), passions (six root), secondary passions (twenty), and indeterminate (four)—fifty-one in all.
