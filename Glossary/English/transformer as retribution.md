# transformer as retribution
> 校准自「maturation transformer」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。


## Explanation（English）
One of the three transformers in Consciousness-Only; the retribution transformer is the first transformer—that is, the transformation of the eighth, ālaya, consciousness. “Retribution” (vipāka; older translation “retribution”) is the general name for the fruit called forth by past wholesome and unwholesome karmic force; this result of retribution is the eighth consciousness itself. The retribution transformer is thus the ālaya's transforming.
