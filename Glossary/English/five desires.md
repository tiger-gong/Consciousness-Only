# five desires
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*pañca-kāmaguṇa*

## Explanation（English）
Two accounts. First: the five kinds of craving that arise from attachment to the five sense-objects—visible form, sound, scent, taste, and touch. See the *Mahāprajñāpāramitā-śāstra*, fascicle 17. Second: desire for wealth, sexual form, fame, food-and-drink, and sleep. Distinct from the object-particular mental activity “desire” (chanda, aspiration): the five desires chiefly name greedy attachment to the five dusts of the desire realm, or to five worldly affairs.
