# hostility
> 校准自「resentment」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*upanāha*

## Explanation（English）
The second of the minor concomitant passions among the mental activities. Resentment is grudging enmity: harboring ill will without letting go, causing extreme passion. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is hostility? Preceded by anger, harboring ill without relinquishing, binding enmity as nature; it obstructs non-hostility and has the activity of burning distress—for those who bind hostility cannot forbear and are constantly heated with passion.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
