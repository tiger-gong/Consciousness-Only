# immediately antecedent condition
> 校准自「immediate-antecedent condition」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*samanantara-pratyaya*

## Explanation（English）
When the preceding moment of mind and mental activities ceases, the condition that equally and without interval opens the way for the arising of the succeeding moment of mind and mental activities.
