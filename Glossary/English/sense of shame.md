# sense of shame
> 校准自「embarrassment (regard for others)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*apatrāpya*

## Explanation（English）
One of the good mental activities among the mental activities. Embarrassment is the sense of propriety: feeling unable to face others for having done wrong is called embarrassment. Cheng weishi lun says: “What is embarrassment? Relying on the power of the world, it has slighting and rejecting violence and evil as nature; counteracting lack of embarrassment and stopping evil conduct as function.” Like shame, embarrassment can stop evil conduct. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
