# desire (aspiration)
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*chanda*

## Explanation（English）
Also called joyful desire: the mental function of aspiration and desire, hoping for the task to be done. The Consciousness-Only school takes it as an mental activity with specific objects arising toward an object of wish. Desire has the three natures of good, evil, and neutral. Wholesome desire is the basis that gives rise to a diligent mind; among unwholesome desires, craving another’s wealth is craving, one of the fundamental passions. Desire has many kinds, such as the five desires, six desires, and three desires.
