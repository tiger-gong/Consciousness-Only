# torpor
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*styāna*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant passion among the mental activities. Torpor makes the mind unclear toward objects—heavy and dull—obstructing serenity and vipaśyanā (insight). The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is torpor? Causing the mind toward objects to lack fitness as nature; it obstructs serenity and vipaśyanā as activity.'
