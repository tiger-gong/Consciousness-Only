# serenity
> 校准自「pliancy (ease)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*praśrabdhi*

## Explanation（English）
One of the good mental activities among the mental activities. Ease and lightness of body and mind is called serenity. Cheng weishi lun says: “Ease means serenity: free from coarseness-and-heaviness, regulating and refreshing body and mind, fitness for the task as nature; counteracting dullness and transformation of the basis as function.” Coarseness-and-heaviness refers to the passions of craving and delusion; the practitioner subdues passions and leaves coarseness-and-heaviness—this is a necessary condition for cultivating meditative concentration. “Transformation of the basis as function” means turning away from coarseness-and-heaviness and relying on serenity. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
