# result of retribution
> 校准自「matured fruit」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vipāka-phala*

## Explanation（English）
The indeterminate retribution induced by retribution-causes (impure good and bad karma), such as the total retribution-body of beings in the six destinies—hence retribution-fruit.
