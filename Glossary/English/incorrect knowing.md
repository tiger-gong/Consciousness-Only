# incorrect knowing
> 校准自「non-introspection (incorrect knowing)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*asaṃprajanya*

## Explanation（English）
One of the fifty-one mental activities of Yogācāra: the mental function of wrongly understanding the observed object; one of the eight major concomitant passions. Non-introspection obstructs right knowing and has the activity of transgression, thus corrupting the precepts. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is incorrect knowing? Toward the observed object, erroneous understanding as nature; it obstructs right knowing and has the activity of transgression.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
