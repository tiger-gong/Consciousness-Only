# nondetermined mental activities
> 校准自「indeterminate mental factors」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*，权威未直接用此词形，依同组体例推衍。

## Explanation（English）
Regret, sleep, investigation, and examination—four whose moral nature (wholesome, unwholesome, or neutral) is indeterminate, hence called indeterminate.
