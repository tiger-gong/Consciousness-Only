# calming-and-insight
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*śamatha-vipaśyanā*

## Explanation（English）
Calming (śamatha) is causing the mind to dwell without discrimination, still and settled, on the object taken as support. Insight (vipaśyanā) is reasoned examination and inferring-and-measuring of that object. The two assist each other: calming can draw forth insight; insight can protect calming. In Consciousness-Only, torpor obstructs insight and agitation obstructs calming. When cultivating calming one must dwell without discrimination; when cultivating insight one relies on applied and sustained thought.
