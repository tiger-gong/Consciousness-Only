# harmfulness
**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vihiṃsā*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant passion among the mental activities. Harmfulness is injuring: the mind without compassion harms sentient beings—the opposite of harmlessness (ahiṃsā). The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is harmfulness? Toward sentient beings, a mind without compassion, injuring and vexing as nature; it obstructs harmlessness and has the activity of oppressing and afflicting—for the harmful oppress and afflict others.'
