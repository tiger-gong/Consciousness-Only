# emptiness of dharmas
> 校准自「selflessness of dharmas」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*dharma-nairātmya*

## Explanation（English）
One of the two selflessnesses, the counterpart of “absence of self.” It means that within all dharmas, conditioned and unconditioned, there is no substantial “self.” That is, all dharmas arise dependent on condition as cause, are mutually dependent, and lack any substantial nature: conditioned dharmas have function yet do not abide permanently; unconditioned dharmas abide permanently yet have no function. “All dharmas are without self” is also one of the three Dharma-seals, called the seal “all dharmas are without self”; the three Dharma-seals are a crucial teaching of the Buddha-Dharma, and whether a teaching is ultimate is verified by these three. To understand that all dharmas arise from condition as cause and truly have no self-nature or substance is the bodhisattva's path of contemplation, whereby one severs the obstacle to the knowable. The wisdom that thoroughly comprehends this principle is called the wisdom of the two selflessnesses.
