# memory
> 校准自「mindfulness (recollection)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*smṛti*

## Explanation（English）
One of the mental activities with specific objects among the mental activities. Mindfulness is memory: clearly remembering without forgetting things one has experienced is called mindfulness. Cheng weishi lun says: “Toward a formerly familiarized object, it has the nature of making the mind remember clearly without forgetting, and the function of being the basis of samādhi.” All realms contacted by the senses, or doctrines thought through, are formerly familiarized objects; the memory-function upon them is the self-nature of mindfulness. “Basis of samādhi as function”: by recollecting formerly familiarized right principles, keeping them ever present, right samādhi arises—that is its function. Toward an object never experienced, recollection does not arise. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
