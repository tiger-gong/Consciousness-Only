# sustained thought
> 校准自「sustained scrutiny (vicāra)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*vicāra*

## Explanation（English）
A nondetermined mental activity. Sustained scrutiny is examining: fine, deep reflection on principles and phenomena. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'Scrutiny means examining: causing the mind to be hurried, toward mental-verbal objects, fine turning as nature.' Inquiry is coarse turning; scrutiny is fine turning—both cause the mind to turn hastily upon mental-verbal objects.
