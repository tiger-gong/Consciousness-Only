# impure seeds
> 校准自「contaminated seeds」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
“Impure” means involving passions and defilement. Impure seeds are the capacity to give rise to impure dharmas, contrasted with pure seeds. The innate and newly perfumed impure seeds in beings' ālaya-consciousness, when they meet conditions, give rise to defiled activity.
