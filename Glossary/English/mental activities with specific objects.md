# mental activities with specific objects
> 校准自「object-specific mental factors」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
Desire, resolve, mindfulness, samādhi, and wisdom—five that each arise by cognizing a specific object-domain, not present in every mind like the universal ones.
