# solitary-head consciousness
Also named unaided mental consciousness: the sixth consciousness arising alone, not together with the first five consciousnesses, ranging widely over the eighteen elements. There are further three: the solitary-scattered consciousness, the solitary-head consciousness in dream, and the solitary-head consciousness in concentration. The “mind-speech object” on which applied and sustained thought rely is the object taken by this solitary-head consciousness through names-and-speech, not borrowing the eye, ear, and the rest of the first five.
