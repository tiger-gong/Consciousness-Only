# vigilance
> 校准自「carefulness (non-laxity)」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*apramāda*

## Explanation（English）
One of the fifty-one mental activities of the Consciousness-Only school; the opposite of carelessness. The mental function that guards against evils and focuses on cultivating the good is called carefulness. The Abhidharma-kośa school takes carefulness as the mental activity for the arising of all wholesome minds; the Consciousness-Only school likewise takes it as a wholesome mental activity. See Cheng weishi lun, fascicle 6.
