# obstacle to the knowable
> 校准自「hindrance to the knowable」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*jñeyāvaraṇa*

## Explanation（English）
Rooted in grasping at dharmas, it obstructs bodhi (awakening) and screens the objects that ought to be known so they cannot be known as they truly are—called the obstacle to the knowable. Bodhisattvas must sever it to become buddhas; the two vehicles do not.
