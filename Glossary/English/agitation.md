# agitation
> 校准自「restlessness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*auddhatya*

## Explanation（English）
A major concomitant passion among the mental activities. Restlessness is mental unsettledness—agitated floating excitement that obstructs meditative concentration. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is agitation? Causing the mind toward objects to be not tranquil as nature; it obstructs equanimity and śamatha as activity.' Śamatha is 'calm abiding,' i.e., samādhi. See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
