# obstacle of the passions
> 校准自「hindrance of afflictions」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kleśāvaraṇa*

## Explanation（English）
The passions rooted in grasping at a self, which obstruct nirvāṇa and screen beings from liberation, are called the obstacle of the passions. Both the two vehicles and bodhisattvas must sever it.
