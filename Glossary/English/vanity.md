# vanity
> 校准自「haughtiness」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*mada*

## Explanation（English）
A minor concomitant passion among the mental activities. Haughtiness is arrogance arising from worldly fame and gain, with proud self-inflation. The Cheng Weishi Lun says: 'What is vanity? Toward one's own prosperities, deeply arising defiled attachment, intoxicated pride as nature; it obstructs non-vanity and has the activity of serving as a basis of defilement—for the intoxicated with pride grow all defiled dharmas.' See Cheng Weishi Lun, fascicle 6.
