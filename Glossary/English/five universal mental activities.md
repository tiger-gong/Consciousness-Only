# five universal mental activities
> 校准自「five universally active mental factors」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

## Explanation（English）
Attention, contact, feeling, conception, and volition—five that can arise in all minds (the eight consciousnesses), hence called universal.
