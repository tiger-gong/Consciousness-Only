# thought of enlightenment
> 校准自「mind of enlightenment」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*bodhicitta*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit bodhicitta, fully “the mind of anuttarā-samyak-saṃbodhi”—the awakened mind that seeks Buddhahood above and transforms beings below; it is the fundamental aspiration aroused by a Mahāyāna practitioner.
