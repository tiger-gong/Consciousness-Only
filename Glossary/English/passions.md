# passions
> 校准自「affliction」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*kleśa*

## Explanation（English）
Sanskrit kleśa, referring to all the confusions and deluded imaginings that disturb body and mind and cause beings to transmigrate in birth and death; the root ones are craving, hatred, delusion, and so forth. Consciousness-Only establishes six fundamental passions and twenty secondary passions.
