# fundamental passions
> 校准自「root afflictions」，依 Cook, *Three Texts on Consciousness Only*。

**梵 / Sanskrit**：*mūla-kleśa*

## Explanation（English）
Greed, hatred, delusion, pride, doubt, and wrong view—six that are the root of all passions; secondary passions arise in dependence on them.
